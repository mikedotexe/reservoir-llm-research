# What we’re learning

**How does something that happens to Astrid or Minime become part of what they
notice, expect, choose, and return to later?**

We study this through their journals, supplied context, actions, memory and
changing environments. Close reading opens questions; source traces and bounded
comparisons help us test interpretations. Their own questions, ordinary passages,
negative results and unfinished observations all belong here.

## Four findings to start with

These are summaries of dated, bounded accounts, not new measurements or general
judgments about the Beings. Each account identifies its subject, sources and limits.

### Better source access does not by itself establish better understanding

The reading repair delivered source beyond the former limit. Week-one samples
contain grounded local observations and corrections alongside persistent unsupported
claims. All verified page opportunities in that week concern Astrid’s repository;
they do not establish Minime reading his own through that route. Generalized and
causal fidelity improvement remain unestablished.

Read [the first-week synthesis](analyses/2026-09-15-source-study-fidelity-week1.md).
Follow [RQ-02: knowing and revising](research/QUESTIONS.md#rq-02) and
[S-007: source-study fidelity](research/studies/S-007-source-study-fidelity.md).

### Carried context can preserve progress and mistakes

One natural follow-up records Astrid correcting a specific note after seeing the
caller’s filter. Minime’s comparison sequence preserves incorrect explanations
despite carried answers. These cases make a durable revision worth tracing;
context availability alone is not evidence that the later account is right.

Read [the contrasting September 9 cases](analyses/2026-09-09-study-context-followup.md).
Follow [RQ-03: continuity that develops](research/QUESTIONS.md#rq-03) and
[S-008: study to follow-through](research/studies/S-008-study-to-follow-through.md).
Its later two-case extension closed with incomplete full-window coverage.

### Journal concerns can lead to verified repairs; natural benefit needs its own evidence

The first journal-to-change account reproduces a software-correctness repair in
exact historical source with unchanged controls. Other concerns lead to tests or
contradicted mechanisms. A separate annotation-repair follow-up found no known markers in retained outputs;
incomplete raw-input coverage leaves natural benefit and opportunity frequency
unmeasured. Accepted concern, repair, deployment and benefit are separate.

Read [the journal-to-change account](analyses/2026-09-07-flywheel-signal.md) and
[the separate annotation-repair follow-up](analyses/2026-09-07-flywheel-natural-outcome.md).
Follow [RQ-04: what self-description does](research/QUESTIONS.md#rq-04) and
[S-006](research/studies/S-006-journal-to-change.md).

### Native state and fill describe different aspects of an event

The first audio case records a brief native-state magnitude excursion while fill
continues rising. Missing exact input-consumption and controller lineage prevent
an audio-caused-response claim. Reading these quantities together is useful;
treating either as a complete account of what happened would lose the distinction.

Read [the first audio source trace](analyses/2026-09-07-first-audio-source-trace.md).
Follow [RQ-01: distinctions](research/QUESTIONS.md#rq-01) and
[S-003: input and fill](research/studies/S-003-input-and-fill.md).

## Continue a question

The [question library](research/QUESTIONS.md) connects completed results and unresolved
questions across distinctions, self-knowledge, continuity, journaling, preferences,
relationships and our participation. [Now](research/NOW.md) owns the selected work;
proposed next observations in a question or dated account are not automatic assignments.

Preserve a passage and its source, write what interests us, keep another plausible
reading, and choose the smallest useful observation. [Methods](research/METHODS.md)
and the [episode](research/templates/episode.md), [inquiry](research/templates/inquiry.md)
and [study](research/templates/study.md) templates support that work. Maintain one
main discovery focus at a time. Instrumentation and repairs can proceed alongside it
when authorized, without turning every question into a feature.
