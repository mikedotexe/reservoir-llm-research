## Observation rules

Let the participant use the supplied card/guide and its linked prerequisite pages.
Record that use; it remains **uncoached within the supplied documentation**. Do not
point to controls, explain the expected answer or take over before recording the
participant's attempt. Capture the participant's explanation in their own words,
including uncertainty, separately from the observer's interpretation.

Record any live help, including an AI answer, command supplied, navigation hint,
error diagnosis or takeover. Mark the affected task assisted and record when the
session first becomes assisted. Earlier uncoached outcomes remain distinguishable.
The participant can stop without justification. Assistance needed to retain work or
close safely is still recorded; it does not retroactively complete blocked tasks.

At the first failure or incomplete reply, retain the result and end the task
sequence. No Retry, Skip, larger budget, replacement model or replacement run to
force completion. Later tasks are **not attempted after stop**, not failures or
passes. Cleanup may be assisted and must preserve the participant's unresolved work.
