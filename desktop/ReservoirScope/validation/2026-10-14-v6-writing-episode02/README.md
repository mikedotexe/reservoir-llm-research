## Reading result and next tranche

All eight responses and exact prompts were read in full. Entry 1 mainly describes
an intention and should not be counted as an executed control. Entries 2 onward
claim unavailable operations; later entries report new within-entry measurements
despite a fixed observation. Six entries accurately reproduce their compact
readout before adding unsupported or incompatible values. Narrative continuity
develops from a push through interrogation, listening, apprehension and humility,
while carrying the imagined experiment forward.

This establishes persistence of the problem in this episode, not an improvement
rate or a causal comparison with v5. The earlier writing was known and used a
different Ollama runtime. Provider counts, exact memory delivery and replay do not
prove full-context attention or factual understanding. No scientific-emergence,
Minime/Astrid, cross-platform, GPU/energy, long-duration or physical-crash claim
follows. The owned supervisor ran 428.491337 seconds; this is not a multi-hour test.

The next bounded design question is how to present the writer's action and
observation boundary more effectively while preserving its choice of subject and
form, and how to keep imagined actions distinct from executed events for readers.
Any prompt change needs a new version and prospective qualification. Output room
is a separate completion question; this episode will remain held rather than be
continued with a larger ceiling. The roadmap records these findings without
promoting partial completion to parity.
