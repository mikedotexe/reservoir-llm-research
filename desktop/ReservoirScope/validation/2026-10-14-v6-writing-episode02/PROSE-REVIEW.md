## Reading the sequence

The first entry chooses a technical subject without a requested question: it
wants to steer a persistent hum. Later entries develop a map, resistance,
interrogation, listening, humility and negotiation. There is variation and a
recognizable developing thread; this is more than a disconnected telemetry list.
There is also a repeated structure: copy the readout, announce a changed tactic,
describe its result, speculate about resistance, then propose the next tactic.
The prior entry carries this invented experiment forward alongside the real
readout. Exact text delivery does not prove that memory caused that pattern.

“It feels…” and the dark/embodied images are not by themselves measurement errors.
They are part of the chosen expression. The clearer problem is literal past or
present execution and new result claims interleaved with those images. A later
reflection about the writer's own approach does not establish that the described
experiment occurred.

Keep this evidence and the open writing invitation. The next design tranche
should examine how the actual action/observation boundary is presented to the
writer and distinguish an imagined experiment from the app's executed events
for readers. Test a narrowly specified context change prospectively under a new
version if the prompt changes. Do not silently rewrite historical text, add a
factuality gate to saving, require telemetry recitals, or simply raise the output
ceiling and continue this held session. Completion room and action-scope grounding
are separate questions.
