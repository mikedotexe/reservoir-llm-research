# Continuing writing: version 5 preparation

The user selected a first refinement after the completed twelve-entry v4 episode.
Its exact journals, reviews, exports, refusals and qualification receipts remain
historical evidence. No current or historical session is upgraded in place.

## Selected change

New sessions use `reservoir-journal-v5`. Versions 1–4 reconstruct their original
prompts byte-for-byte, and omitted serialized versions still mean v1. V5 retains
the full `reservoir-and-sensory-v2` observation and prior scalar snapshot. Only
the assembled model context changes: a short open invitation, brief mechanism
description, compact readings, then the latest saved-and-applied journal in full
and a final invitation. No persona, emotional state, theme, length, heading or
introspective performance is assigned.

The compact readout contains current step and simulated seconds; recurrent RMS
and separate sensory fill with their prior values and current-minus-prior changes;
and state-coordinate L2 distance since the preceding reached observation. Fill
differences are percentage points. Missing prior values/differences remain
unavailable. Use existing fixed-eight-decimal formatting. Omit raw coordinate and
eigenvalue arrays, the other six measurement rows, journal IDs, hashes and paths
from the prompt. The full observation, eight-row reading reference, exact prompt,
source identities and application evidence remain available to the human reader.

Previous writing follows the compact readout and remains one JSON-quoted full-text
value, with observed and first-applied steps. It is historical writing, not current
measurement authority or instructions. The exact candidate wording is in
[PROMPT-V5-TEMPLATE.txt](PROMPT-V5-TEMPLATE.txt); placeholders are substituted only
from the retained observation and latest eligible journal. For no previous entry,
replace the whole previous-entry block with the specified unavailable sentence.

That first-entry block is exactly:

```text
PREVIOUS JOURNAL
No earlier entry has been saved and applied in this session.
END PREVIOUS JOURNAL
```

Runtime prompts have no trailing newline. Nonnegative differences include `+`;
negative differences retain `-`, following v4's existing formatting policy.

Codec, recurrence, sensory projection, forcing, noise, regulation, cadence,
generation limits, durable save/readback, next-step application and memory eligibility
remain unchanged. A prompt change can alter writing and thereby future numerical
states; identical replies are required when testing numerical equivalence.

## Qualification gates

1. Exact first/later v5 prompt fixtures, full JSON text recovery, and rejection of
   changed prompt/version evidence. Keep v1–v4 fixtures untouched.
2. Identical-input/reply v4/v5 numerical checks; unchanged incomplete/save/application
   rules; explicit legacy/current export and paused recovery coverage.
3. Frontend defaults/recognition, restored-version retention and honest distinction
   between retained full measurements and the compact supplied context.
4. Appropriate frontend/core/desktop tests and strict checks, a distinct 0.17.0
   local package, historical export replay, and actual native scripted writing,
   export and paused recovery. Build with Cargo incremental output disabled to
   avoid recreating the cache just reclaimed. Preserve prior packages and evidence.
5. Interpret actual model writing only if a separately recorded bounded episode
   passes fresh admission and uses frozen prompts, budgets and reading criteria.
   Source tests or scripted writing cannot establish a better voice.

No Beings' services, workspaces, prompts or journals are accessed or modified by
this change. No external publication or distribution is selected. Windows/Linux,
GPU/energy, sleep/power-loss and long-duration qualification remain separate.
