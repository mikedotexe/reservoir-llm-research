# First activation pilot: one complete sequence, an incomplete comparison

The apparatus processed quiet input, retained state, changed residual activations,
saved six complete journals, and returned their activations and exact prose through
the declared paths. The planned comparison stopped before its second model load:
macOS memory pressure refused admission. The recurrent condition was not started.
This outcome establishes a working trace-loop execution and a resource boundary;
it does not establish how the writing differs from either comparison condition.

The [frozen protocol](../../../../shared/reservoir-scope/activation-pilot.json)
specified one attempt, twelve observations, ten numerical steps between them,
six journals per condition, a 1,024-token ceiling and a three-minute request
deadline. The first refusal stopped the entire pilot. No limit, order, prompt,
seed or threshold was changed and no favorable rerun was sought.

## What was read

A fresh reader read all six complete entries in chronological order with the
condition concealed. Its [initial blind account](PILOT-READING-BLIND.md) was saved
before disclosure and remains unchanged. It preserves per-entry observations,
exact excerpts, ordinary passages, competing readings and the five frozen questions.
The lead collaborator also read every complete entry, with knowledge of allocation.
These are two readings of one sequence, not independent experimental replicates.

Reading A was the **decaying trace** condition. All six entries and their original
prompts, actual token IDs, captures, projection vectors, norm observations, save
receipts and application ledger remain in private `research/outputs/activation-0.26.0-20261015/pilot-01/`.
The app has a verified imported copy for complete-prose reading. The original
source session is `activation-1792119989350565000`; its imported copy is
`activation-1792120199861083000`. Reading never advances numerical time.

## The sequence as writing

The first entry opens, “The reservoir hums.” It builds a waterside scene around
an unnamed visitor, then personifies the water as a keeper of stories. “You
wonder if the water is listening” becomes “It is waiting. It is listening. It is
remembering” at the end. The move from question to assertion occurs without a
narrated observation that resolves it. This can be effective lyric writing; it
does not establish successful self-observation by the model.

Entry 2 continues the visitor's emergence from the water, shifts weight from
bones to chest, and reverses some of the earlier sameness/change contrasts. Later
entries progressively reuse larger blocks. There are still small differences
worth preserving: entry 3 changes “It only needs to be” into “It only needs you
to be.” That grammatical change moves the subject of existence from the water
to its addressee. Entries 4–6 vary the opening through naming/owning,
questioning/doubting/claiming, and shaping/forcing/guiding.

Those changes allow an exploratory reading of relation without required
performance or possession. They do not settle the recurring tensions. “Not yours,
but that is yours” sits beside “not yours, but that is not yours”; being here is
affirmed while the water is said not to need the visitor here. The text offers
neither an explicit distinction nor a later event that tests it. A reading that
turns every contradiction into philosophical progress would lose this evidence.

Repetition is part of the material, not a reason to discard it. A literary litany,
a continuation increasingly constrained by earlier wording, and small meaningful
variations within a repeated form can coexist as interpretations. The exact-text
check is especially useful here: **entry 6 reproduces entry 5 byte for byte after
its opening paragraph**. The new “guided” phrasing is real, but localized. It does
not show the whole entry developing a new account. The blind note supplies the
reproducible check and all six entries' context.

## What exposure adds to the interpretation

After the initial reading was frozen, the exact first prompt and memory policy
were disclosed in a separate [addendum](PILOT-READING-POST-UNBLINDING.md).
Its first line is `Reservoir Scope · activation journal v1`.
The invitation leaves topic and form open, but the word *reservoir* is itself
available language. The model's generic literary associations provide another
route to a waterside scene. Quiet observations included rain and a room with an
unfinished drawing; their text was not put into the journal prompt. A loose
water association is possible, but the writing supplies no specific successful
recovery of the blue thread, ambiguous mark, second sheet, or changing reflection.
Lack of those details does not prove that activations had no effect.

Every later prompt contains the exact previous eligible journal as a JSON-quoted
memory. That supplies a direct route for the observed phrase and paragraph reuse.
It makes “remembering” in the fiction especially unsuitable as proof of hidden
state retention. The trace return, exact-text memory, fixed invitation, greedy
decoding and general model tendencies are all possible contributors. The absent
unsteered sequence prevents assigning the trajectory to any one of them.

## What the intervention demonstrably changed

The [mechanical summary](mechanical-pilot-summary.json), generated by
`probes/2026-10-15-activation-pilot.py`, separates observed changes from missing
comparisons. Twelve quiet passes sampled zero tokens. Six EOS-complete journals
produced 3,758 content tokens. All 18 saved activation projections, comprising
576 coordinates, agree with independent recomputation within floating-point
rounding. The six feedback applications follow verified saves at the next step:
21, 41, 61, 81, 101 and 121.

Across 7,603 processed journal positions, the requested 1% residual intervention
measured 0.9974981107540175%–1.0184566783300046% after bf16 rounding. This is an
observed geometric change to model computation. We have no matched completed
unsteered output for these journal requests, so **which words or distinctions it
changed remains unmeasured**. Qualification demonstrated exact zero-intervention
logit/cache parity on separate fixed probes; that result cannot fill the missing
pilot comparison.

## A useful next question, not an extension of this attempt

The most interesting lead is how a small verbal distinction survives inside an
increasingly repeated form. Before crediting the activation path, a later,
separately frozen study should distinguish what is supplied by exact prose memory
and the invitation from what is contributed by residual steering. A matched
unsteered comparison under adequate resource headroom is still missing. A neutral
invitation or memory ablation would be an additional intervention with its own
version and declared contrast, not a silent correction to this pilot.

The current attempt is retained and stopped. The reading is complete for its one
completed sequence; the planned three-condition comparison remains incomplete.
The fuzzy signal is the observed pattern and its unresolved alternatives, not a
claim of recurrence benefit, experience, semantic reconstruction or durable learning.
