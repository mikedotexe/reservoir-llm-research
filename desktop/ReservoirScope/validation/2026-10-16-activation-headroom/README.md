and a separate serial cohort path can share one admitted model across fresh
condition records. The new attempt passed memory admission on this Mac and
retained two unsteered journals. Its third response entered a sentence loop and

The live systems were left untouched. No service unload, model reconfiguration,
system purge, process-name kill or research delivery was performed. Earlier
stopped attempts and retained inference binaries remain unchanged. This work
is committed locally; no new publication is claimed here.

## The one new attempt

free percent. Three healthy readings arrived after approximately 120 seconds;

under the predeclared rule, without admitting another model load. Execution
stopped after 76.55 seconds on the first incomplete response, well inside the

occurred.

not a wholly context-naive reading. The separate

## Remaining question and evidence

The next prospective design decision is how a bounded incomplete writing outcome
should affect collection of the other controls. A new protocol could record such
an outcome for its condition and still collect independently bounded conditions;
that would be a declared change to the stopping rule. This attempt remains stopped.
Title and exact-prose-memory contrasts are also still separate unanswered questions.
