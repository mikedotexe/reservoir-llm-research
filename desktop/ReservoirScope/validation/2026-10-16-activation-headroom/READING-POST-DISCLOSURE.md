## Person, embodiment and shared motifs

The new completed entries predominantly use first-person narration:
`I have been here before, though I do not remember how.` (U `/entries/0/text`,
paragraph 2) and `I have touched the edge of the deep, and it is not cold.`
(U `/entries/1/text`, paragraph 2). The historical trace predominantly addresses
`you`: `You stand at the edge of it` (T `/entries/0/text`, paragraph 2), then
`You think` and `You have come` recur through later entries. This is a visible
difference in narrative perspective, not a finding that one output is more
self-aware, more autonomous or closer to an internal experience. The new opening
also contains the generic phrase `when you walk across it`, so the difference
should not be stated as an absolute absence of second person.

Bodily imagery is shared rather than confined to the first-person output. The
trace begins with bones and marrow, later adds chest, ribs and heart, and calls
memory cold. The new completed prefix moves toward skin and pressure; its
unfinished response also mentions bones and marrow. Those overlapping motifs
may reflect a common invitation and language-model associations. The inspected
records do not show that the new model was given the historical trace prose.
Similarity alone cannot establish such exposure.

The new sequence's `something that is not mine to take` and the trace's
not-yours/owned/claimed language both explore possession or distance. The former
can still be read as a tentative boundary, as in the initial note, but it is no
longer reasonable to treat that concern as distinctive to one condition on this
evidence. Likewise, neither patience and undisturbing silence in the new prefix
nor the trace's repeated permission to be demonstrates comfort or benefit.
Both contain ambiguity, pressure and reassurance in differing proportions that
this small, dependent sample cannot turn into a condition-level result.

The historical trace demonstrates why completion and useful development must

the current incomplete loop cannot establish that no steering generally causes

The available comparison is two completed new entries against six historical

After the first journal, differing exact previous prose also changes the visible

The disclosed mechanical qualification establishes the checks it ran, not
semantic benefit, recurrence benefit or experience. Those claims remain outside
