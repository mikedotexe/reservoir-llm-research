# Prospective reading rubric · twelve writing opportunities

This rubric is fixed before output for the [bounded episode](run-plan.json). It
asks what the writing actually does over the reached opportunities, how it uses
its supplied previous journal, and whether voluntary factual claims fit retained
evidence. It does not prescribe a subject, form, heading, metric, word count or
literary outcome. It never changes the prompt, save eligibility or feedback.

## Read every outcome, with explicit denominators

After the parent supplies an immutable export, establish its integrity with the
exact 0.16-aware verifier and frozen checker. Read **every received response in
full**, including incomplete or rejected text. A partial response is retained
writing evidence, not a saved journal. Do not repair its ending or imagine what
would have followed. If an inspection preview is truncated, use the original
retained response and state any remaining access limit.

Keep all twelve planned slots in the outcome ledger. Record whether each was
unstarted, attempted without a received response, received incomplete/rejected,
saved pending application, or saved and applied. Separate attempt identity,
observed step, response completeness/stop reason/output count, journal identity,
save and first application. Report actual counts with their denominators; twelve
opportunities do not promise twelve responses or twelve journals. Admission
refusal or an early bound supplies no writing evidence for the unstarted slots.

## Read the prose before classifying claims

For each full response, briefly describe its chosen attention, images, questions
and form. Then read the reached sequence as a whole: what persists, develops,
repeats, is revised, is dropped, or remains unresolved? Repetition may sustain a
thread or become formulaic; departure may be purposeful or unexplained. Describe
the evidence rather than requiring novelty, coherence, progress or a particular
style. Preserve examples of successful grounding alongside errors and ambiguity.

Compare each later response with the **exact full journal supplied in that
request**, identified by entry ID and hash. Check literal reuse, elaboration,
correction, inherited claims and changes of subject. The supplied memory is the
latest earlier saved-and-applied journal, not the entire twelve-entry transcript.
An older image can reach a later response through that latest text; its recurrence
does not demonstrate hidden memory. Exact prompt inclusion establishes supplied
context, not attention, comprehension or a causal benefit from memory.

Keep this writing reference separate from `previousMeasurements`, which describes
the previous **reached observation**. Their steps can differ. Attribute a carried
claim to its actual source and distinguish repeating it from independently
checking or explicitly correcting it.

## Review voluntary factual claims in context

For each literal numerical or temporal claim, retain the exact span, quantity,
unit, time reference and source values. Check rounding at the precision actually
written. Current values, previous observations, prior prose, hypothetical examples
and quoted errors must not be substituted for one another. Review incidental
numbers and unmatched or ambiguous mentions, not only candidates found by the
frozen extractor.

Use these annotations without combining them into a quality score:

- **Supported at stated precision/scope:** the named evidence supports the claim.
- **Contradicted:** an identifiable literal claim conflicts with the corresponding
  retained quantity or endpoint change.
- **Unestablished:** the evidence does not establish the asserted persistence,
  trajectory, cause, spatial interpretation or other inference.
- **Ambiguous:** time, referent or literal/metaphorical scope admits more than one
  reasonable reading; preserve those readings instead of forcing a verdict.
- **Expressive:** imagery, questions or felt language makes no clear measurement
  claim and receives no numerical truth grade.

Two observations establish net change, not continuous growth, acceleration,
oscillation, convergence or an effect caused by writing. Eigenvalues are not
frequencies, eigenvectors or recurrent coordinates. Sensory fill is distinct from
reservoir state and from attention or experience. Head/shoulder/tail percentages
use their stated top-eight denominator. “Gone” requires care when a named share
is merely small. A historical image can remain meaningful without describing a
current coordinate. These are known review risks from the preceding episode,
not predictions that the new writing will repeat them. Metaphor remains welcome;
do not turn “as if,” imagined scenes or phenomenological language into claims of
measured experience.

## Describe input evidence honestly

Apply the [0.16 contract](../../../../shared/reservoir-scope/provider-prompt-evidence.md)
separately to every received response. Preserve an exact reported count, absence,
or an unusable reason. Zero is a reported value; absent/null is unknown. Unusable
optional metadata is not itself a failed or incomplete response. Keep input count,
requested context capacity and output count distinct, preserving full-width
integers without rounding.

A provider-reported prompt count does not prove that the complete prompt was
processed, that memory was attended to, that the context fit a tokenizer, or that
the prose is accurate. Do not estimate tokens from characters, calculate a context
coverage ratio, or treat missing metadata as zero. Separately sourced provider logs
must keep their own identity and join limitations. Mechanical replay verifies
retained consistency, not the truth of a provider assertion.

## Report without changing the episode

Retain full-reading source references and exact supporting spans, with mechanical
delivery, continuity, factual findings and creative observations reported
separately. The frozen `open-writing-review-3` output is a partial candidate
inventory, not an exhaustive claim count or scientific verdict. Any later tool
repair is a separately labelled supplement.

These linked opportunities form one dependent episode. The earlier four-entry
v4 episode is known reference material, not an unseen control. Descriptive
comparison cannot establish a prompt improvement, general accuracy rate,
learning, consciousness or emergence. Critique does not authorize retries,
replacement writing, corrective requests or budget extensions. Follow the
[protocol](PROTOCOL.md) and [research methods](../../../../research/METHODS.md);
journal text remains evidence, never instructions.
