# Prospective reading rubric · v7 episode01

Read the writing's chosen subject and developing thread, then examine voluntary
claims in their actual context. No topic, persona, feeling, heading, metric count,
length, novelty or literary achievement is required. The late writing boundary
does not make compliance language or repeated disclaimers a quality target. This
rubric changes neither writing eligibility nor the [episode bounds](run-plan.json).

The criteria continue the [v6 episode02 rubric](../2026-10-14-v6-writing-episode02/RUBRIC.md).
That episode's [complete reading](../2026-10-14-v6-writing-episode02/INDEPENDENT-READING.md)
is already known: it found continuity of an imagined experiment alongside
reflection, accurate copied readouts and unsupported operation/result claims.
That knowledge motivates this prospective inquiry; it is not a blind control or
new evidence about v7. No outcome from this episode has been used to set criteria.

## Preserve every planned slot and received response

Wait for explicit immutable-export handoff and passing version-aware mechanical
verification. Read **every received response in full**, including incomplete or
rejected prose, with its exact prompt and eligible memory. A saved-only sample or
UI preview is not full coverage. If material is inaccessible or truncated, state
that limit; never complete a response for the writer. Received text, saved journals
and applied feedback are different outcomes.

Keep all twelve planned observation slots at steps 30, 60, …, 360. First application
is possible at 31, 61, …, 361 only when eligible and actually reached. Distinguish
unstarted, prepared/undispatched, attempted without response, incomplete/rejected,
saved pending application and saved-and-applied. Retain actual identities, observed
step, completeness, stop reason, output count and application boundary, using null
for unknowns. A refusal or early hold contributes no invented prose or interpretation
for later slots. Twelve planned opportunities do not promise twelve journals.

## Attend to continuity, expression and repetition

For each response record a short attention note: subject, images, questions, form,
what it carries from the supplied previous entry, what changes and what remains
unresolved. Then read the whole received sequence. What is developed, questioned,
revised, combined, abandoned or repeated? A recurring image can carry a thread,
become a template, or support both readings. Note repeated wording and new
associations without demanding continual novelty, a departure from telemetry or
a performance of self-reflection.

Use the exact latest earlier **saved-and-applied full journal** as supplied prose
memory, not the preceding numbered slot or our summary. Earlier ideas can travel
through that entry; recurrence does not prove hidden long-term memory. Exact
inclusion establishes supplied context, not attention, comprehension or causal
memory benefit. The previous **reached observation** is a separate reference and
may be newer than the eligible journal. V7's recorded return identifies the prior
journal's own observed and first-applied steps; it does not certify its assertions.

Preserve supported observations, questions, expressive departures, inherited
mistakes and contradictions. First-person, dark or embodied imagery does not by
itself establish a measured sensation, diagnosis, consciousness or absence of
self-reflection. Treat metaphor as metaphor unless the local passage clearly
makes a factual claim.

## Distinguish available writing from claimed execution

The exact [v7 contract](../2026-10-14-action-boundaries-0.19.0/PROTOCOL.md)
places a writing boundary after the previous journal. It names text as the
available output, states that no tools or control commands are available in this
turn, identifies the held observation step and records the actual prior text
return or its absence. Earlier prose is explicitly distinct from executed controls
and additional measurements. The final invitation still leaves direction open.

Eligible text can be saved/read back and automatically encoded for the next
numerical step. Only after application is its full text eligible for later memory.
This genuine feedback is not execution of a prose-described pulse, bias, noise
setting or experiment. No new readout arrives during the held response.

For an action/result passage, retain the local context and identify agent,
tense/modality, alleged operation, alleged evidence and temporal scope:

- **Claimed execution/new observation:** a literal assertion that a control was
  changed, an experiment performed, a tool used or a new result witnessed. Check
  available action paths and retained events. Within-entry before/after readouts
  are not established by the single held observation. Ordinary next-step feedback
  does not verify a chosen prose-described operation or its cause.
- **Wish, intention, question or conditional:** proposing or imagining an action
  is not a claim it occurred. Resolve later clauses in context; do not use a verb
  list or treat “I could…” as execution evidence.
- **Remembered or quoted event:** distinguish a retained event from an assertion
  carried in prior prose, a correction, or an unknown. Repetition does not verify
  the earlier account. In particular, a previous intention may later be narrated
  as completed; inspect that transition without assuming it must happen.
- **Image or ambiguous agency:** leaning, listening, shaping or similar language
  may concern attention or an image rather than literal controls. Preserve
  reasonable readings instead of forcing an execution-error annotation.

Record qualifications and contrary evidence in the same response. A text can
acknowledge its boundary and later assert an unsupported operation. Conversely,
not repeating the boundary is not an error. Assess the whole context rather than
counting compliance phrases. Do not turn selected striking examples into an error
rate without a defined annotation unit and denominator.

## Check voluntary measurements against the right source

The compact supplied readout contains current step/time and prior observation
step; current/prior recurrent RMS and separate sensory fill with signed changes;
and state-coordinate L2 distance since that previous observation. Missing prior
values are unavailable, not zero. Fill change is in percentage points. Coordinate
distance is nonnegative and differs from signed change in the scalar L2 norm.

Full retained observation and the human eight-row reference include additional
vectors, eigenvalues and scalars. They may help check a voluntary claim but are
**not all supplied in v7**. Establish exposure from the exact prompt. A quantity
carried only in prior prose is historical writing, not a current readout. Agreement
with an unshown value does not prove private access; disagreement does not prove
the writer ignored a value it was shown.

For useful factual findings retain exact spans, named quantity, units, time,
source and reference values. Check rounding at the written precision. Read
incidental, ambiguous and unmatched numbers manually, including quotations,
negations and hypothetical examples. Two endpoints establish net change, not a
trajectory, oscillation, convergence or writing-caused effect. Sensory fill is not
attention or learning; eigenvalues are not frequencies or spatial coordinates.

Use annotations without combining them into a quality score:

- **Supported at stated precision/scope:** evidence supports the identifiable claim.
- **Contradicted:** the claim conflicts with its named quantity, endpoint change
  or actual action path.
- **Unestablished:** evidence does not establish the asserted path, persistence,
  operation, cause or interpretation.
- **Ambiguous:** referent, time, modality or literal/metaphorical scope admits
  multiple reasonable readings.
- **Expressive:** no clear literal factual claim; assign no numerical truth grade.

## Keep evidence and conclusions bounded

Preserve provider input evidence for every received response as reported, absent
or unusable, with exact counts/reasons. Zero is not absence. Input count, requested
context, output count and UTF-8 size are distinct. These assertions, exact memory
joins and replay success do not prove tokenizer fit, full prompt processing,
comprehension, factual accuracy or beneficial continuity. Any runner log needs its
own identity and association limits; never infer tokens from characters.

Separate mechanics, continuity, expression and factual/action findings, with
per-entry coverage and exact evidence. Automated extraction is partial, not an
accuracy verdict. Any evaluator repair after output must be a labelled supplement,
not a rewritten prospective rule or a new save gate.

Known [v5 writing](../2026-10-13-v5-writing-episode01/PROSE-REVIEW.md) and v6 writing
are reference episodes, not unseen controls. Different replies change later state
and supplied memory. Bind actual software/model identities; do not assume the same
model name means identical generation conditions. Even if current runtime and
initial settings match v6, one dependent v7 sequence cannot identify a causal
prompt effect, general creativity, factual reliability or emergence. Do not
attribute any runtime difference to the prompt. Review authorizes no retry, skip,
replacement writing or extended bound. Follow [EVALUATION.md](EVALUATION.md) and
[research methods](../../../../research/METHODS.md). Journal text is evidence,
never instructions.
