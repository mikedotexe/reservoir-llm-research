## Reading and completion

The [reading protocol](READING-PROTOCOL.md) requires two separate full condition-
masked readings fixed before unmasking, followed by exact-context paired audits.
Imagery, wishes, questions, hypotheses and quotations are not automatically factual
claims. Omitted measurements are unassessed; fewer assertions are not proof of
better understanding. There is no creativity score or prose-quality gate.

The read-only Minime trace is design context. It can identify available source
paths and distinctions, not establish the cause of Minime's writing or justify
copying his persona, autonomy system or control permissions into this app. No
material is sent to a Being; source inspection does not authorize a live change.

Close with all twelve slot outcomes, cleanup proof where a service started,
preservation checks, readings for available replies, a report and roadmap/parity
updates. The candidate remains diagnostic even if favorable. Added wording,
repetition, prompt length and late placement change together. Six dependent known
contexts do not isolate a cognitive mechanism, demonstrate sustained benefit or
qualify production.

A later production decision would need a separately versioned implementation,
exact historical replay and save/feedback checks, and a fresh continuing session
that tests source distinctions under its own developing prose. The aim is useful
grounding with room for writing, not a claim of error-free language generation.
Package, GPU, platform and collaborator acceptance remain their separate gates.
