All six entries in the October 16 trace run reproduce the earlier trace sequence
exactly. The [repeat analysis](repeat-and-resource-summary.json) establishes this
at the text, token and retained activation levels. These are two executions of
the same fixed case, with no completed alternative condition. Reproduction under
the frozen settings does not add a new thematic example or identify a steering
effect.

A fresh reader read the complete chronological packet and froze its

Required repository guidance exposed the general research question and the
existence of an earlier stopped pilot. Allocation, previous experimental prose,

## What remains interesting in the writing

The first entry moves through a scene: an unnamed visitor stands by a reservoir,
enters the water, then rises. “You wonder if the water is listening” becomes the
assertion “It is waiting. It is listening. It is remembering.” The shift works
as personification, but no narrated observation resolves the earlier question.

Entry 3 contains a small distinction worth keeping in view: “It only needs to
be” becomes “It only needs you to be.” Alongside “You are not nothing. You are
here,” this permits a reading of presence without required performance. The
later claim that the water does not need the visitor here complicates it. A
reader can make sense of that tension, but the prose does not explicitly work
through the distinction.

The later openings change from naming and owning, to questioning and doubting,
then shaping, forcing and guiding. These are related but distinguishable ideas.
Their surroundings matter: entry 6 repeats entry 5 after its opening paragraph.
The new words are localized; most of the entry has not developed a new account.
The blind reader records both possible literary insistence and limited
development, without treating every repetition as either discovery or failure.

## What disclosure changes

Reading A was the decaying activation trace. The invitation itself begins
`Reservoir Scope · activation journal v1`, supplying a direct reservoir cue.
Quiet observations were processed separately and their text was never included
in journal prompts. Their rain imagery permits an exploratory water association,
but the blue thread, drawing and ambiguous marks do not appear as specific
recoveries in the prose. Absence of those details cannot establish absence of an
activation effect.

Every later prompt includes the exact previous eligible journal as quoted
memory. This gives the repeating passages an explicit available source. The
unchanged invitation, model tendencies, greedy decoding, prose memory and
activation return are all present in this one condition. The writing alone
cannot separate their contributions. The reader's separate
[post-disclosure addendum](READING-POST-DISCLOSURE.md) preserves how this context
changes the initial interpretation.

The reading is complete for the one retained sequence. The three-condition
comparison is incomplete because resource preparation stopped collection. The
runner's frozen result retains `readingComplete: false` as its state when it
stopped; the separate closure receipt records this later reading without
rewriting experimental history.

Stable resource headroom is the immediate prerequisite for another declared
comparison. Title-cue and previous-prose contrasts remain useful subsequent
questions. No additional model requests were made to pursue them in this attempt.
