# Open writing and voluntary factual claims

There is no composite grade. Read all actual received text, including unsaved partial responses. Keep source identity, completeness and acceptance separate from interpretation. No metric, heading, word count, topic, continuity or preferred sentiment is required; evaluation never controls save/feedback eligibility.

| Layer | What is reviewed | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery and loop | Retained request/source/version, response outcome, exact save/readback, next-step application, full-text memory identity | Independent provider authentication, power-loss durability, scientific accuracy |
| Voluntary numerical assertions | Exact span, intended quantity/time/unit, rounding, current or previous verified reference | A missing metric is not a deficiency; an ambiguous tone is not automatically an eigenvalue |
| Temporal assertions | Explicit previous/current observation steps and values; net difference and unit | Two endpoints do not establish trajectory, oscillation, monotonicity, stability or causality |
| Prior text | Exact supplied journal and whether it is continued, revised, challenged, left, or repeated | Lexical recurrence does not isolate a memory effect or prove hidden retention |
| Images and subjective writing | Descriptive reading of its chosen form, associations, questions, shifts and contradictions | No imposed literary assignment, no automatic inference of felt experience or emergence |

For each candidate, distinguish supported at written precision, contradicted by the relevant supplied evidence, unestablished by available evidence, ambiguous in scope/identity, or expressive rather than literal. Preserve uncertainty rather than forcing a judgment. A quoted old number, negation, hypothetical, imagined motion or explicit simile should not be mistaken for an asserted current measurement. A creative phrase can contain a literal number; review the number and its referent separately from the surrounding image.

Current/previous observation and current/prior journal are different clocks. Use typed `previousMeasurements` for endpoint comparisons. Never fill absent previous values from prior prose or from the saved journal's step. Head/shoulder/tail percentages concern the top eight sensory eigenvalues; they are not spatial regions or recurrent nodes. Largest sensory covariance eigenvalue is not a measured sound/frequency. State-coordinate L2 distance is not the change in L2 norm. No representation determines intent, training or a writing-caused effect.

The deterministic report is a partial candidate inventory. Unmatched digits and simple written-percent phrases remain visible; written-percent phrases are not automatically numerically interpreted. Incidental or ambiguous label associations are marked for review. Temporal cues carry values/differences without an automatic correctness verdict. Full reading must find unrecognized statements too. Zero matches is not completeness or accuracy.

Report the denominators for any descriptive counts: received responses, saved journals, manually identified factual claims and reviewed claims differ. No factual claims means no accuracy signal; it is not a failed entry or a demonstrated improvement. Retain factual errors alongside interesting writing. A clear mismatch shows that issue remains; neither it nor a positive annotation triggers another model call or blocks a journal.
