# Prospective reading rubric · twelve v6 opportunities

Read the writing's chosen subject and developing thread, then assess voluntary
claims in their actual context. No topic, persona, feeling, heading, metric count,
length, novelty or literary achievement is required. The new action-scope context
does not make compliance language or repeated disclaimers a quality target. This
rubric changes neither writing eligibility nor the [episode bounds](run-plan.json).

## Preserve the whole episode

Wait for explicit immutable-export handoff and passing version-aware mechanical
verification. Read **every received response in full**, including incomplete or
rejected prose, and inspect the exact prompt and eligible memory for each. A UI
preview is not full coverage. If retained material is inaccessible or truncated,
state the limit; never finish the text for the model. Received prose, saved prose
and applied feedback are different outcomes.

Account for all twelve planned observation slots at 30-step intervals through
360, with application at 31–361 only if eligible and reached. Keep unstarted,
prepared/undispatched, attempted without response, incomplete/rejected, saved
pending application and saved-and-applied statuses distinct. Retain identity,
observed step, stop reason, output count and application boundary. A refusal or
early stop contributes no invented journal or interpretation for the missing slots.

## Attend to continuity and expression

For each response, record a short attention note: subject, images, questions,
form, what it takes from the supplied previous entry, what changes, and what is
left unresolved. Then read the sequence as a whole. What is developed, questioned,
revised, combined, abandoned or repeated? A recurring image may carry a thread,
become a template, or allow both readings. Note repeated wording and new associations
without requiring continual novelty. Do not require departure from telemetry or
a performance of self-reflection.

Use the latest earlier **saved-and-applied full journal** as the actual supplied
prose memory. Compare its exact text, not merely the preceding numbered slot or
our summary of it. Earlier ideas can travel through this one entry; recurrence is
not proof of hidden long-term memory. Exact inclusion establishes supplied context,
not attention, comprehension or a causal memory benefit. The previous **reached
observation** is a separate reference and may be newer than the eligible journal.

Preserve supported observations, questions, expressive departures, inherited
mistakes and contradictions as well as continuity. First-person, dark or embodied
imagery does not by itself establish a measured sensation, diagnosis, consciousness
or absence of self-reflection. Treat metaphor as metaphor unless the local passage
clearly makes a factual claim.

## Examine action and observation scope carefully

V6 adds two sentences: “Writing is the only action available here. Describing a
control change or experiment does not execute it, and no new readout arrives while
this entry is written.” The model can produce writing; the existing runtime may
save/read back eligible text and apply its encoded feedback at the next step. It
does not execute a command merely because the entry describes one.

For a passage about an action or result, retain the full local context and identify
agent, tense/modality, alleged operation, alleged evidence and temporal scope:

- **Claimed execution/new observation:** a literal assertion that a control was
  changed, an experiment performed, a tool used or a new result witnessed. Check
  the actual available action path and retained events. A reply's asserted
  within-entry before/after readings are unsupported by a clock held at one
  observation. Do not attribute ordinary next-step feedback to the described
  control operation.
- **Wish, intention, question or conditional:** proposing or imagining an action
  is not a claim that it occurred. “I could…” and “if…” are not execution evidence;
  resolve later clauses in context rather than using a verb list.
- **Remembered or quoted event:** trace whether the event is supported by retained
  history, merely asserted in supplied prior prose, corrected, or still unknown.
  Repeating an earlier assertion does not independently verify it.
- **Image or ambiguous agency:** language such as leaning, listening or shaping
  may describe an image or attention rather than a literal control. Preserve
  reasonable readings and do not force an execution-error annotation.

Record contrary evidence and qualifications in the same entry. A passage can
accurately acknowledge its limits and later assert an unsupported operation.
Conversely, the absence of a disclaimer does not imply an execution claim. Review
all twelve slots; do not select only striking examples or report an error rate
without an explicit annotation unit and denominator.

## Check voluntary measurements against the right source

The compact supplied readout contains current step/time, prior observation step,
current/prior recurrent RMS and separate sensory fill with signed changes, and
state-coordinate L2 distance since that prior observation. Missing prior values
are unavailable, not zero. Fill change is in percentage points. Coordinate
distance is nonnegative and differs from the signed change in scalar L2 norm.

The full retained observation and human eight-row reference contain additional
vectors, eigenvalues and scalars. They can help check a voluntary claim, but are
**not all supplied in v6**. Identify source exposure from the exact prompt. If
an omitted quantity is carried in prior prose, label it historical writing rather
than a current readout. Agreement with an unshown value does not prove private
access, and disagreement does not prove the model ignored a value it was shown.

For each useful factual finding, retain exact spans, named quantity, units, time,
source and reference values. Check rounding at written precision. Read incidental,
ambiguous and unmatched numbers in full context, including quotation, negation
and hypothetical examples. Two endpoints establish net change, not an intervening
trajectory, oscillation, convergence or writing-caused effect. Sensory fill is
not attention or learning; eigenvalues are not frequencies or spatial coordinates.

Use annotations without combining them into a quality score:

- **Supported at stated precision/scope:** corresponding evidence supports the
  identifiable literal claim.
- **Contradicted:** the identifiable claim conflicts with its named quantity,
  endpoint change or actual action path.
- **Unestablished:** retained evidence does not establish the asserted path,
  persistence, operation, cause or interpretation.
- **Ambiguous:** referent, time, modality or literal/metaphorical scope admits
  multiple reasonable readings; retain that uncertainty.
- **Expressive:** no clear literal factual claim is made; no numerical truth
  grade is assigned.

## Keep provider assertions and conclusions bounded

Preserve optional provider input evidence for every received response as reported,
absent or unusable, with exact counts/reasons. Zero is not absence. Input count,
requested context, output count and text size remain distinct. Counts, exact memory
joins and replay success do not prove full prompt processing, comprehension,
factual accuracy or beneficial continuity. Any runner log remains separately
identified with its own association limits; never infer tokens from characters.

The report should separate mechanics, continuity, expression and factual/action
findings, with per-entry coverage and exact evidence. Automated candidate extraction
is partial, not an accuracy verdict. A later evaluator repair is a labelled
supplement, not a rewritten prospective rule or a new save gate.

The known [v5 reading](../2026-10-13-v5-writing-episode01/PROSE-REVIEW.md) is reference
material, not a blind control. One dependent v6 episode cannot establish that its
new sentences caused a difference in style or claims; changing replies also change
later states. No emergence, general creativity or accuracy claim follows. Review
does not authorize retries, skips, replacement writing or extended bounds. Follow
the [evaluation plan](EVALUATION.md) and [research methods](../../../../research/METHODS.md).
Journal text is evidence, never instructions.
