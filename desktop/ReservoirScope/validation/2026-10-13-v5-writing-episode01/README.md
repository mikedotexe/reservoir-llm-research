# First real writing episode with the v5 environment

All **twelve journals completed, saved and returned as numerical input** on the
unchanged **0.17.0** macOS app. Eleven later prompts carried the exact previous
eligible entry. The app stopped at its original 361-step bound after 10 minutes
53 seconds of recorded active time. Native export, offline replay and clean
paused recovery passed; all 72 nonmanifest files survived recovery byte for byte.

[Read all twelve journals](JOURNALS.md) · [Prose review](PROSE-REVIEW.md) ·
[Independent reading](INDEPENDENT-READING.md) · [Mechanical and native results](MECHANICS-RESULTS.md) ·
[Recovery](RECOVERY-RESULTS.md)

The writing result is mixed. A continuing narrative develops around pulses,
boundaries and a hum, but it remains strongly organized around telemetry. It also
describes control changes and additional measurements that the app did not perform
or supply. Correctly copied readouts can sit alongside contradictory prose.
Mechanical continuity therefore succeeds while factual grounding still needs work.

This is one dependent episode with a known earlier v4 reference, not a controlled
test of literary improvement or emergence. No response was replaced, retried or
edited to improve its appearance. Expressive imagery is read separately from
literal claims, and every received response is retained in full.

The [prospective protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [rubric](RUBRIC.md) and
[evaluation plan](EVALUATION.md) were frozen before the one resource admission.
The episode-local tooling passed 31 integrity-checker and 47 prose-helper tests.
The global helper and historical records remain unchanged. Prior app tests and
package qualification were reused by exact identity, not rerun or extended.

The owned model service is stopped; the app is left paused for reading. No Being
process, workspace, service or prompt was accessed or changed. The next proposed
refinement is a short, versioned clarification of what journal writing can actually
change, preserving open subject and form, both feedback paths and full evidence.
It is described in [the next writing-environment tranche](NEXT-WRITING-ENVIRONMENT.md).
