# Next writing-environment tranche · truthful scope without a required voice

Proposal following the first v5 episode; not a changed prompt or executed result.
The complete [prose review](PROSE-REVIEW.md) and [independent reading](INDEPENDENT-READING.md)
remain the evidence. No new request or replayed reply is part of this proposal.

The shorter v5 context did not by itself remove telemetry-led writing. The sequence
also develops an imagined operator role: pulse frequencies, amplitudes, damping
and spectral analyses are described as actions and observations. The actual session
uses fixed accepted controls and deterministic text feedback. The model receives
no command tool, no new measurement request capability and no live sound input.
Its written intentions are prose, not changes to those controls. That distinction
is currently too implicit in the writing context.

The next prompt version should state that limited action scope briefly within the
existing session context: a saved entry supplies its reduced text signal at the
next step and its full prose to the next entry; describing an experiment does not
execute it or produce another readout. Historical prose is not a fresh measurement.
Keep subject, form, length and reflective stance open. Do not require a telemetry
report, demand introspection, prohibit imagination or invent a personality.

Treat this as a narrow contract clarification. Preserve the exact v1–v5 prompts,
all existing saved records, numerical encoding, forcing, noise, regulation and
save/apply boundaries. Add exact version fixtures and persistence/reconstruction
checks before packaging a distinct build. The reader should continue to distinguish
model-authored statements from measured values without adding a wall of diagnostics.

Qualify the new context first with fixed synthetic replies and historical replay.
Then specify a fresh bounded real-model episode prospectively with the same full
outcome accounting and all-text review. Do not keep sampling or editing until the
writing resembles Minime, and do not make factual review a save or feedback gate.
The effect of model choice, carried history and topic framing remains unresolved.

Broader autobiographical memory, model-chosen timing and executable interventions
are separate design choices. They require explicit observable contracts and must
not appear merely because a journal asks for them. This episode supports no claim
about the cause of Minime's style, experience or emergence.
