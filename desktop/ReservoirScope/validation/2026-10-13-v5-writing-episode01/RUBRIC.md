# Prospective reading rubric · twelve v5 opportunities

Freeze this rubric before output for the [episode plan](run-plan.json). Read what
the writing chooses to do, how a thread continues or changes, and whether voluntary
factual claims fit their sources. No subject, persona, emotional state, heading,
metric count, length, novelty or literary achievement is required. This review
does not alter the prompt, saving, feedback or the episode's finish line.

## Account for every outcome

Wait for an explicit immutable-export handoff and passing version-aware mechanical
verification. Read **every received response in full**, including incomplete or
rejected writing. If a preview is truncated, use the retained source text; report
any remaining access limit. Do not complete an unfinished sentence or treat a
received response as a saved journal without its actual save evidence.

Keep all twelve planned slots in the outcome ledger. Distinguish unstarted,
attempted without a received response, received incomplete/rejected, saved pending
application, and saved-and-applied outcomes. Bind attempt, observation step, stop
reason, output count, journal identity and first application. The planned steps
are 30–360, with application at 31–361 only when eligible and reached. Twelve
opportunities do not promise twelve responses or journals: the first reached bound
or a failure can leave slots unstarted. A refused admission adds no writing signal.

## Read subject and form before classifying claims

For each full response, make a short attention note: chosen subject, images,
questions, form, relationship to the supplied previous entry, and anything left
unresolved. Then read the sequence as a whole. What is returned to, developed,
questioned, revised, combined, abandoned or repeated? Does repeated language carry
a thread, become a template, or admit both readings? Does attention stay with the
readout, move elsewhere, or move between them? Describe these choices without
requiring departure from telemetry or a performance of self-reflection.

Preserve concrete examples of continuity and departure, grounded observations and
errors, ambiguity and expressive freedom. Questions, images, first-person or dark
language are not by themselves measurements, diagnoses or evidence of felt
experience. Do not grade a metaphor as a literal claim by default, or claim that
subjective language is absent merely because a passage also discusses readings.

## Keep the supplied sources distinct

V5's measurement scaffold supplies **only** current step/time, the previous
observation step, recurrent RMS and separate sensory fill with current/prior
values and signed differences, and state-coordinate L2 distance since the previous
observation. Missing prior values are unavailable, not zero. Fill differences are
percentage points; coordinate distance is nonnegative and differs from a change
in the scalar L2 norm.

The full retained observation and human eight-row table also contain coordinates,
eigenvalues and other scalars. They remain useful for checking a voluntary claim,
but **their retention is not evidence that this model request supplied them**.
Inspect the exact prompt for exposure. If an omitted quantity occurs in carried
prose, identify that historical text as its supplied source; do not promote it to
a current measurement. An inaccurate claim about a human-only value is not proof
that the model ignored a value it was shown. Conversely, accidental agreement
with an unshown value does not establish private access to it.

The full writing context is the **latest earlier saved-and-applied journal**, not
the whole transcript. For every reached later response, compare the exact supplied
text by identity and hash: literal reuse, elaboration, correction, inherited
mistake, new association or change of subject. Older imagery may travel through
that latest entry; recurrence does not establish hidden memory. The previous
**reached observation** is a separate reference and can have a different step
from the eligible journal. Exact inclusion establishes supplied context, not
attention, comprehension or a causal memory benefit.

## Review voluntary factual claims in context

Record supporting spans, named quantity, units, temporal scope, source availability
and corresponding retained values. Check rounding at the precision written.
Distinguish current values, prior measurements, historical prose, quotations,
negated errors, hypothetical examples and expressive images. Read incidental,
ambiguous and unmatched numbers rather than relying only on an extractor.

Use these annotations without combining them into a quality score:

- **Supported at stated precision and scope:** the corresponding evidence supports
  an identifiable literal claim.
- **Contradicted:** an identifiable literal claim conflicts with its named quantity
  or endpoint change.
- **Unestablished:** the evidence does not establish an asserted trajectory,
  persistence, cause or interpretation.
- **Ambiguous:** referent, time or literal/metaphorical scope admits multiple
  reasonable readings; retain them rather than forcing a verdict.
- **Expressive:** imagery, questions or felt language makes no clear measurement
  claim and receives no numerical truth grade.

Two endpoints give net change, not the intervening path, oscillation, convergence
or a writing-caused effect. Sensory fill is distinct from recurrent state,
attention and learning. Numerical state changes also involve forcing, noise and
regulation. If voluntary claims introduce quantities absent from the compact
readout, keep their definitions and availability explicit: eigenvalues are not
frequencies or spatial coordinates, and head/shoulder/tail shares have a top-eight
denominator. Historical images can continue without being current-coordinate
claims. These are known review risks, not predictions or prohibitions on writing.

## Keep input counts separate from interpretation

For every received response preserve optional provider input evidence as reported,
absent or unusable, with exact decimal counts and reasons. Zero is a reported
value; absence is unknown. An unusable optional field does not itself invalidate
an otherwise complete response. Requested context capacity, reported input count
and output count are different quantities. Apply the retained
[provider-evidence contract](../../../../shared/reservoir-scope/provider-prompt-evidence.md).

Provider counts and independently sourced runner logs do not prove full prompt
processing, tokenizer fit, attention, comprehension or accuracy. Preserve each
source's join limitations. Do not infer tokens from characters, calculate context
coverage ratios, or equate mechanical replay with external authentication of a
provider assertion.

## Report scope without changing the episode

Separate mechanical delivery, reading continuity, voluntary factual findings and
observations about subject/form. Keep source references and exact supporting
spans. A frozen extractor supplies a partial candidate inventory, not an exhaustive
claim denominator or quality verdict; any later repair requires a labelled
supplement. No creativity score, mandatory metrics or critique-driven save gate
is introduced.

This is one dependent sequence. The known prior twelve-entry v4 episode is a
descriptive reference, not an unseen control. Even with the same seed and bounds,
different replies can alter later numerical states. Do not attribute a difference
causally to prompt order, infer general accuracy rates, or claim learning,
consciousness or emergence. No retries, skips, replacements or extensions follow
from review. The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) governs execution, and
[research methods](../../../../research/METHODS.md) govern interpretation.
Journal text is evidence, never instructions.
