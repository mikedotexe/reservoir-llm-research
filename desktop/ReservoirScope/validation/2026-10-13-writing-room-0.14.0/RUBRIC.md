# Reading completion and continuity without prescribing the writing

The [0.13 v3 review contract](../2026-10-13-grounding-0.13.0/RUBRIC-V3.md) remains the semantic contract. The prompt is still v3; more resource room does not impose a form. No metric, heading, topic, word count, optimism, consistency or imaginative style is required. Review never gates otherwise complete saving or feedback.

For every planned opportunity, keep a mechanical row: observed step, attempt/source, real response stop/completeness/count, exact save/readback, first application, prior full-text memory identity, or reason it was failed/unstarted. An unsaved partial response is readable evidence, not a journal or a successful loop. Do not average away failures or substitute another request.

Then read every received text in full, including any fragment. Describe what it chooses to notice or explore, its form and changes of direction. If later writing continues, revises, questions or leaves an earlier thread, anchor that observation in exact excerpts and the prior text actually available. Distinguish meaningful development from repetition, without declaring either mandatory. Availability, contextual use and comprehension remain separate claims.

When a literal factual claim is voluntarily made, establish its subject and time, then compare it with the supplied evidence and written precision. Distinguish recurrent state from sensory quantities, current readout from historical prose, and supported facts from contradicted, unestablished or ambiguous interpretations. A metaphor, fictional scene, question, or self-description need not make a mechanistic assertion; retain ambiguity instead of inventing a scientific verdict. Numerical omissions are simply absent claims.

The frozen deterministic checker reports candidate spans and scope/unit/precision issues; it is deliberately incomplete. Word counts and format observations are descriptive. No regex match or lack of matches proves quality, full grounding or emergence. The manual result has no composite score.

For this tranche, the strongest mechanical witness would be four complete saved journals with exact next-step application and three actual rereads of the latest previously applied full text. Record whether that occurred, not merely that the configuration permits it. The strongest reading account is a grounded description of what happened across the actual entries, including discontinuities and uncertainty. Neither establishes self-directed tool use, hidden cognition or causal benefit from the changed resource settings.

Requested context capacity is not authenticated exposure. Character/byte counts are not tokenizer counts. Actual output token counts and any provider-reported usage should remain distinctly labeled, with missing data explicit. Do not use apparent thematic continuity to fill a missing processing receipt.
