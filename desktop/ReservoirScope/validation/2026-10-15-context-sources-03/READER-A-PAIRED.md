## Interpretation

The candidate keeps writing open in form; it produces a short proposed action at 30,
multi-paragraph reflection at 60/90, and longer sensory continuations later. It does
not force explanatory prose in every case. Spring, hum, grey, metal, containment and
dissolution recur across both arms because they share the same historical memory.
Each B response is independent and never becomes another B request's memory, so
this is continuity with supplied prose, not a developing candidate voice.

At 60 the observed candidate is less telemetry-heavy and avoids A's time claim.
At 120 and 360 it introduces unsupported numerical observations; at 360 those are
especially authoritative-looking. The explicit distinction added late in the prompt
therefore has no reliable benefit across these examples. No production promotion is
warranted from this diagnostic. This is an implementation decision about the tested
candidate, not a prose-quality gate or a requirement for error-free writing.

No reply establishes an actually executed tool/control operation. There is still
affordance confusion and asserted geometry, which should remain distinct from
execution claims. Metaphorical scent, sound, emotion and strange imagery are not
defects by themselves. These known dependent cases cannot establish general
performance, causality, subjective experience or behavior over a sustained loop.
