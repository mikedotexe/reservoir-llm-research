# Reading-order deviation and additional reader

Root/Reader A and Reader B each fixed their full condition-masked notes before
unmasking. Their original notes and receipts remain unchanged.

While inspecting the two receipt schemas after that point, root printed Reader B's
JSON receipt. Unlike root's short receipt, B's receipt also contained the masked
observations. Some of those observations appeared in root's tool output before
root wrote its paired audit. Root's paired audit therefore cannot be described as
independent of B's first account. This was a review-process mistake, not a change
to prompts, response text, source evidence or generation.

Reader B continued its independent source audit without seeing root's notes. An
additional Reader C was assigned the full masked texts, then required to fix its
notes before unmasking and independently audit each pair before reading any other
account. C had worked on preparation, so knew the proposed diagnostic, but had not
seen responses, mapping or reader notes. Neither additional reading nor this note
caused a new model request. This recovery is post hoc and does not erase the
original deviation.

The report distinguishes the original A/B masked passes, the B/C independent
paired assessments, and root's supplemental synthesis. Masking is limited in all
passes: authored headings and values remain visible, cases were known in advance,
and all readers are agents rather than independent human collaborators.
