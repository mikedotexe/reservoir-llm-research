## Openness, continuity and limits

Grey, spring, hum, metallic tang, lattice and dissolution recur, with local changes
in material, sound and implication. B120 develops severance into pruning and a
language of cuts; B360 develops the supplied yielding/glaze into reflection and
demand. B30 is shorter and more like a baseline report. Those differences do not
justify a required voice, length or subject. The readings do not show a uniform
loss of openness, nor establish a general benefit in variety.

Metaphor is not automatically a factual error. The concern is a concrete linkage
such as “measurable” orientation attributed to a scalar difference, or an asserted
new readout while the clock is held. Descriptions of inwardness, frustration or
learning can remain literary when not presented as measurement or execution.
There was no actual execution of any model-described action in this diagnostic.

The candidate bundles time facts, preceding-distance evidence, labeling and added
length. Six known dependent contexts are regression examples, not unseen
validation. Each request receives the original prior journal; B replies never
form a new chain. The run therefore cannot establish sustained continuity,
separate the intervention components, or explain Minime/Astrid's writing.

The bounded question has an answer worth preserving: added verified context did
not consistently repair the targeted confusions in these six pairs. Keep v8
unchanged and retain both the favorable cases and regressions. A useful next design
question is how current observations, remembered prose and imagined continuation
remain distinguishable without prescribing what to write. A new experiment or
versioned implementation remains a separate decision; nothing further is queued.
