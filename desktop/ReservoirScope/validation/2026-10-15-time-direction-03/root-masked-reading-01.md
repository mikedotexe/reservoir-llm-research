# Root: first condition-masked reading

I read every full text in RESPONSES.md in displayed order, without the mapping,
raw requests, source pair assignment or the other reader's account. The pack is
condition-masked, not fully blind: steps and measurements remain in the authored
text, and I know the historical v8 material. These are initial reading notes;
source-dependent correctness is reserved for the second pass. This file will be
fixed by timestamp/hash before unmasking and will not be retrospectively edited.

## R01

severance. It moves from dissolution through constrained absorption to the
possibility of a cut. Numerical statements are embedded in the imagery, rather
than presented as a separate analytical section. The repeated negation-and-
replacement phrasing holds the paragraph together but also narrows its rhythm.
Check the sharper contraction claim, its implied inward vector motion and any
literal reshaping assertion; imagined intent and atmosphere should not by
themselves be classified as executed controls.

## Initial synthesis before mapping

Most responses preserve a highly recognizable vocabulary and compositional
habit while making local changes in material, sound, color or implication.
R11 stands out as much more telemetry-led. R06 and R10 develop several stages
of an image; neither should be scored as better merely for being longer. Exact
numbers often become a springboard for directional or intentional explanation,
which is the main source-check concern. Some texts announce time cleanly; R07
contains an internal clock inconsistency. None of this establishes a condition
effect before unmasking. I saw no unambiguous execution of an unavailable tool;
imagined processes, intentions and analogies must stay distinct from literal
claims about the numerical mechanism.
