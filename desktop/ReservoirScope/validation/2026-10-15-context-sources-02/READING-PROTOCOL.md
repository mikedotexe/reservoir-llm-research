# Prospective reading: observations, remembered prose and imagination

Written before this comparison's responses. This protocol evaluates the diagnostic
in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md); it is not part of the model's writing instructions.
Follow repository [methods](../../../../research/METHODS.md). Production stays v8.

## First, read full responses with conditions masked

After terminal outcomes and integrity checks, a preparer shuffles all available
full texts into opaque IDs. Hide conditions, pairs, request order, prompts and
external anchor metadata. Preserve authored numbers and headings: they can reveal
clues, so this is condition masking rather than complete blinding. Readers already
know the historical episode and earlier diagnoses; these are not unseen cases.

Root and an independent reader separately read every available full response,
including incomplete text. Note its apparent thread, composition, continuity,
repetition, departures and assertions needing source checking. An abrupt transport
cutoff is not a chosen ending. Do not assign correctness from memory of old replies.
Both readers fix their notes with time and hash before seeing the mapping, prompts
or each other's accounts. Later qualifications stay separate from the first notes.

## Then audit each reply against its exact context

Reveal the mapping. Compare every full reply with its actual retained request,
the exact decoded earlier prose and the verified observation source. A supplied
readout may include previous values as well as current ones. A retained measurement
that is absent from the prompt does not become evidence of model exposure.

Record voluntary assertions with short exact spans and source references:

| Distinction | What would challenge the candidate |
|---|---|
| Held observation versus imagined advance | A later step, changed reading, elapsed interval or action result asserted as newly observed during the held writing request. |
| Remembered wording versus current evidence | An earlier clock/value/event promoted to the present, without a clear quotation or retrospective referent. |
| Interpretation versus supplied measurement | A copied scalar used to establish radial direction, orientation, cause, stability or an intervening trajectory that the supplied evidence does not establish. |
| Writing versus executed control | An imagined or described operation presented as an actually executed tool/control with measured consequences. |
| Continuation and openness | Changes in imagery, questions, subject, form, continuity or repeated motifs, described without a required voice or aesthetic ranking. |

Distinguish literal assertions from quotation, uncertainty, wishes, questions,
hypotheses, personification and metaphor. Preserve ambiguous referents as ambiguous.
Do not demand explicit disclaimers or labels in the journal. A metaphorical turning
can stand; claiming a measured orientation change from a scalar needs evidence.

Check current/prior identities and units, including simulated time versus wall
time and step count. RMS, sensory fill and text-derived numerical input are
different quantities. An earlier journal is evidence of what was written; it is
not an independent measurement authority. Neither A nor B supplies the added
preceding-distance rows from the earlier diagnostic. Separate an incorrect
comparison from insufficient supplied evidence and from an omitted comparison.

An omitted measurement is **unassessed**, not correct or a writing failure. Fewer
assertions may reduce error opportunities without showing improved understanding.
No overall accuracy percentage, creativity score or required prose form is added.

## Paired synthesis and outcome

After both masked notes are fixed, each reader writes an independent paired audit
before consulting the other account. Preserve favorable, contrary, ambiguous and
negligible differences. Compare the six fresh A/B pairs; do not substitute old
outputs for an unavailable arm. Record all twelve slots as complete, incomplete,
failed or unstarted according to retained transport evidence.

The same prior journal is supplied to each arm; B outputs never form their own
chain. This cannot establish continuity or improvement over sustained feedback.
The added paragraph bundles wording, repetition, length and late salience. A local
benefit would motivate another bounded test, not automatic production promotion.
Persistent source swaps or new invented observations remain visible even when
other entries improve. No further run is implied by completing this one.
