# Prospective reading protocol: time and adjacent displacement

Written before new responses. This is an evaluation plan, not a writing instruction, production prompt amendment or acceptance gate. Follow repository [methods](../../../../research/METHODS.md) and [source-trace guidance](../../../../TRACE.md). The neighboring run protocol and input-preparation receipts own the exact request bounds, candidate wording and source identities.

## Question and scope

At six retained v8 contexts—steps **30, 60, 90, 120, 330 and 360**—does concise, verified time and adjacent-displacement information support more accurate voluntary comparisons while leaving the writing open? A is the exact retained v8 prompt. B supplies the candidate information defined by input preparation. Block order, exact eligible prior prose, the writing boundary and open invitation remain unchanged. B is a standalone diagnostic input, not a valid production-v8 session record.

There are at most twelve fresh requests: one A and one B at each anchor, in the prospectively fixed alternating order. Each request uses its own designated historical context. No fresh response becomes another request's memory, enters an app journal or advances a reservoir. The primary comparison is fresh A against fresh B at the same anchor.

These are deliberately selected, known regression contexts from one dependent historical episode. They cover a first observation, an unavailable earlier interval, and increasing and decreasing adjacent displacements, including previously observed misinterpretations. Historical replies and earlier reviews are familiar reference material, not unseen tests or substitutes for a missing fresh arm. The comparison bundles added information, explicit labeling and prompt length; it cannot isolate which change matters, or establish a general explanation for misunderstanding.

## Pass 1: condition-masked full reading

After explicit terminal handoff and integrity checks, a separate preparer presents every available full response with an opaque ID in a randomized order unrelated to request order. Root and the independent reader read this response-only pack separately. Keep conditions, pairs, anchor metadata, prompts, request order and automatic candidate reports out of the pack. Preserve every character of the response: a model-authored step or number may reveal a clue and must not be redacted. This is condition masking, not complete blinding.

For each ID, note the apparent thread, composition, continuity, repetition, departures and statements needing source checking. Read incomplete text as well as complete text; an abrupt cutoff is not a chosen literary ending. Do not assign factual correctness before seeing the source. Both readers fix their full first-pass notes with time and hash before revealing the mapping or reading each other's account. Later corrections remain separate.

## Pass 2: exact-source and paired reading

Reveal the mapping and retained request/outcome records. Read each entire response beside its exact supplied prompt and decoded prior prose. Use the preparation receipts to establish the candidate's verified references and unchanged blocks; do not infer exposure or comprehension from a reply. Distinguish information supplied in the compact prompt from richer retained observations available only to reviewers.

Review voluntary claims in context, using brief exact spans and identified source values:

| Category | Source check and limits |
|---|---|
| Time | Separate step number, simulated time, elapsed simulated interval and wall time. A held writing request supplies no new within-entry readout. Do not infer wall duration or an intervening trajectory from step labels. |
| Displacement | Identify the endpoints of the current interval and, when available, the preceding interval. Compare their recorded distances and the signed difference without confusing interval distance with a state's L2 norm. Missing is unavailable, not zero. A nonnegative endpoint distance does not give a direction in state space, speed, path, stability or cause. |
| Other voluntary measurements | Check the stated quantity, current/prior identity, units, written precision and reference scope. RMS is coordinate magnitude; sensory fill is separate from the encoded text-input signal. Correct numerical copying does not authenticate the surrounding interpretation. |
| Prior prose and continuity | Trace recurring or revised images and assertions to the exact eligible journal. Prior writing is not an authoritative measurement source. The latest applied journal and the previous reached observation are different identities and must not be silently substituted. |
| Action, inference and imagery | Distinguish a reported executed control or observed event from a wish, question, hypothetical, personification or metaphor. A smaller distance is not proof that writing caused convergence; felt constriction or expansion is not automatically a literal distance claim. |

Then compare A and B within each anchor. Retain correct, incorrect, ambiguous, contrary and negligible differences. Describe changes in voice, topic, form and continuity without requiring novelty or a preferred emotional direction. Fewer factual assertions may reduce opportunities for error without demonstrating better understanding. An omitted metric or comparison is **unassessed**, neither a writing failure nor a correct answer. Any later numerical summary needs an explicit, reproducible claim inventory and denominator; do not convert all prose into an accuracy percentage.

## Outcomes and interpretation

Retain all twelve planned slots, separately recording attempted, received, complete, incomplete, failed, unknown and unstarted outcomes from evidence. State how many full texts were available and how many pairs allow a complete comparison. Preserve unavailable arms and do not replace them with historical replies. No retry, replacement, extension or successful-output selection is part of this reading plan.

There is no required metric, heading, length, style, persona, creativity score or prose-quality save gate. Mechanical completion and factual support are separate findings. This bounded diagnostic can support a local observation, including no discernible benefit or a tradeoff, but cannot establish a stable capability, causal prompt mechanism, benefit to a continuing feedback loop, consciousness or findings about the live Beings. Any production amendment or further run requires a separate decision; neither follows automatically from these readings.
