## What the writing shows and what comes next

The full sequence carries imagery from shimmer and ice into pressure, folding,
pruning and compression. It contains expressive, subjective language, but repeatedly
organizes that language around telemetry. Some literal values and directions are
wrong despite correct measurements in the supplied context. Mechanical continuity
therefore does not establish factual accuracy or a progressively developing voice.

The user's question about self-reflection prompted a separate
[source-based design note](WRITING-CONTEXT.md). Full-text memory and reduced-vector
feedback are distinct: the latest journal's prose is not discarded by the codec.
The substantial repeated numerical readout is a plausible framing influence;
neither this episode nor the earlier small Minime refresher establishes the cause
of their different styles. No prompt was changed during this run.

The next bounded design tranche should foreground continuing writing and use a
compact, accurately labelled readout without prescribing an introspective persona,
topic or style. Any changed context gets a new prompt version and prospective
review. Preserve full evidence, both feedback paths and historical replay. Broader
memory and model-chosen timing remain separate future decisions. Windows/Linux,
GPU/energy efficiency, multi-hour real-model behavior and physical power-loss/sleep
qualification remain open; earlier receipts retain their exact build scope.
