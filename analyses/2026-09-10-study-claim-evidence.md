## Execution, review and finish line

The new freezer and read-only reviewer reuse the **unchanged** qualified source-first
runtime. It loads one immutable local Gemma 4 12B 5-bit MLX instance serially on the
previously qualified GPU configuration, with fresh cache/RNG per trial. Settings
remain temperature 0.7, top_p 0.95, thinking off, a 4,096-token ceiling and 32-token
prefill chunks. There is no reservoir coupling. This is a backend substitution for
the historical Minime context, with no live recurrent state or Astrid comparison.

Frozen adapter/helper copies and hashes supplement the existing runtime manifest.
The adapter checks current source identity before each prepared call and after the
run; the existing runtime checks its source blocks, dependencies and model assets.
Fixed inputs are retained before generation, and actual rendered prompts/token IDs
are recorded separately. The reviewer reconstructs exact treatment inputs and checks
native terminal tokens, finish reasons, token counts and cache progression without
independently retokenizing the receipts.

Readiness admission remains a read-only loopback GET with up to 120 seconds waiting;
it does not reserve shared-host capacity. Per-cell guards remain 1,800 seconds and
18 GiB RSS. Every frozen outcome counts: missing, admission refusal, resource/error,
empty, allowance or channel-boundary responses stay separate from nonempty native
EOS completion. Resume only absent frozen trials; never replace an unfavorable result.

An independent reviewer receives only opaque responses, the historical source and
the pre-generation contextual rubric. Input layout, account, seed and pair labels
stay hidden until original annotations are frozen. Content can reveal likely
conditions, so allocation hiding is not guaranteed complete blinding. Root's later
paired interpretation is explicitly unblinded.

Assess fixture purpose, proxy commitments, shown call relationships and proposed notes
separately. Volunteered source/origin attributions are checked only where visible
evidence permits; origins that require hidden inputs remain unassessed until the
join. Exact response and source quotations use distinct fields. Context qualifiers
and genuine ambiguity remain visible, and later disagreement uses an addendum.
No score rewards length, citations, rejection of memory, a note update or a chosen
NEXT. A short response, retained supported account, hypothesis, further question,
continuation or stop is valid. Generated commands and notes remain unexecuted data.

## Evidence and interpretation limits

All eight original independent annotations remain byte-for-byte unchanged and
read-only. Their completion receipt verifies 72 exact claim quotations, one context
quotation, 24 visible-basis quotations, 24 origin quotations, 43 source quotations
and 17 choice texts/payloads. Joining adds allocation identifiers without recoding;
no schema adapter was needed. Selected claims comprise 49 supported, six contradicted

whole-answer accuracy scores or a representative sample of all possible statements.

The blind reviewer leaves Material 1/2/3 ordinal attributions unassessed where their
origin requires hidden input labels. Separate unblinded reviews check them against
the actual cards. The supported responses identify their materials correctly, while
correct identification of a source still does not guarantee that it supports an
inferred relationship. Original coding, exact quotations and later paired readings
are retained separately in the packet.

Completion counts range from 507 to 612 tokens (median 590; total 4,621), including
one terminal token per response and no filtered tokens. All stop well before the
4,096-token allowance; this experiment offers no evidence that a larger allowance
would repair this particular failure. Prompt lengths match exactly within each pair:
5,834 for retained accounts and 5,605 for supported accounts. Generation takes
51.8–172.5 seconds per cell (median 131.6; total 937.5). Admission waiting adds
122.0 seconds in total. These shared-host timings and response lengths are descriptive,
not measures of understanding or isolated provider performance.

This completed negative correction result does not establish that evidence-linked
navigation cannot help. It covers one historical episode, two seeds and a model
substitution. Labels and claim repetition are already shared between layouts;
the experiment cannot estimate their independent effect. Supported controls show
preservation of supplied correct material, not a remedy for the mistaken account.
The note-content difference is worth retaining without promoting a mandatory format
or a live change on this evidence alone.

The current-source consumer trace provides the more concrete next engineering
candidate: exercise a chosen study through selection, provider delivery, Reader
checkpoint and artifact/continuation handling, with correlation across those
surfaces. That would test the boundaries actually shown in code. It is a proposed
follow-up, not an observed production failure or an automatically started patch.
