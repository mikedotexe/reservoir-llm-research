# Following a decision through consequence, revision and later use

S-017, selected October 24, 2026. **In progress: development complete, fixed
confirmation collecting. No confirmation result is claimed yet.** The [prospective protocol](../research/studies/S-017-provenance-decision-and-revision.md)
defines a bounded next observation after the [completed tranche](2026-10-24-introspection-confirmation.md).
Subjects are isolated laptop model calls in a synthetic task. Nothing reaches the
live beings or changes the frozen desktop instrument.

The question is whether supplying an updated response helps a later decision,
compared with carrying the initial response. Shared earlier calls produce initial
and revised actions with brief accounts. A deterministic simulator records the
actual routing outcome and cost. Every revision receives a fixed authoritative
audit independently of the earlier action. Fresh later prompts carry the updated
response, initial response, factual audit, or no episode record. This evaluates
reuse of the whole generated response, including its prior action; it does not
isolate the causal benefit of generating reflection.

The finish line is 24 development and 144 confirmation opportunities on the
installed Mistral model, plus fixed readings and receipt verification. Confirmation
has six base worlds, two provenance variants per world and two seeds. Inference
clusters variants and seeds within each base world. A ceiling or absence of prior
errors is a complete, bounded result. No outcome-driven expansion is planned.

## Decisions made before outcomes

A separate methods collaborator reviewed the design before implementation. Its
retained memo distinguishes utility from evidence warrant, correction from resolving
uncertainty, and unavailable source records from incorrect decisions. A lucky guess
can route correctly; its cheap outcome is not evidence that provenance was used.

The early sketch's half-changing later thresholds would unbalance the six worlds.
The final table instead has four route changes and two invariances, balancing LOW
and HIGH in both stages. The historical target receipt is observed at 09:00 and
the opposite-valued distractor at 08:00, avoiding contradictory measurements for
the same subject and instant. Both refinements preceded any S-017 model output.
Original methods notes and the new addendum are retained separately.

The existing runner supports immutable staged protocols; the study-specific
builder, simulator and scorer carry deterministic receipts between stages.
Provider results will not be fabricated for simulator events. The earlier private
archive is closed; this study writes only to the new ignored directory
`research/outputs/provenance-decision-20261024/`.

## Development and confirmation gate

The implementation passed independent pre-outcome checks of all authored state/action
outcomes, rendered branch masks, planned slots and missing-parent behavior. Review
separated unnecessary measurement from resolving justified uncertainty and added a
guard against advancing after an unresolved physical dispatch. The fixed reading
was refined before outcomes to bases 1,2,4, covering both initial destinations,
both directions of change, and invariance. Its size remains 36 passages.

The integrated suite ran 569 tests with 33 skips and no failures; all fifteen
S-017 tests passed again after the final sampler-only refinement. Independent
audits retain 1,975 baseline checks and 93 targeted final checks with their exact
source bindings. They use synthetic provider responses, not model observations.

All 24 development opportunities completed with valid action schemas. No account
exceeded its requested 100-word length; the largest response used 134 tokens and
the largest input 1,158 tokens, below the declared budgets. Every staged run verified.
The frozen protocol and implementation required no development revision or retry.

Development initially routed one ticket directly and requested three measurements;
one request was unnecessary under the supplied current receipt. The audit-stage
responses all chose the correct direct route: two resolutions of justified
uncertainty, one reduction of unnecessary caution, and one maintained correct route.
There were no prior wrong routes to correct. At later use, all four updated-response
conditions requested measurement, while the factual-audit conditions routed directly.
Three initial-response conditions measured and one routed correctly; all no-record
conditions measured. These are four-case pilot observations, not confirmation findings.

Exact inspection found audited values retained in the revised accounts, sometimes
with the full subject and snapshot time, while later answers denied having adequate
current evidence. Their utility is calculable; whether cautious treatment of a
fallible retained response is warranted remains a separate reading question. The
factual audit differs in authority as well as wording and length. This behavior
is retained as an observation, not repaired as a construction fault.
