## The design assumptions that failed

An exact byte delivery can still be a poor reading surface. In the frozen S-008
window, eleven Astrid READ_MORE pages delivered 43,271 bytes, including 20,274
bytes of complete ANSI sequences. The heading did not identify the selected
document. Two pages spent more than 3,800 of their 4,000 bytes on terminal controls.
Those are representation and navigation problems, not a reason to fault the
Being for discussing the material actually supplied.

A recalled account can also sound like freshly inspected code. Minime generation
`1788915699263-dcd36fd6` received a map and an earlier response excerpt; it
introduced two symbols absent from the matched WIT revision. Astrid generation
`1788915770498-84971-128` received his account and repeated one. Delivery success
and peer attribution alone were not enough to distinguish those evidence sources.
This remains one selected propagation case, not an error-rate estimate.

The [original account](2026-09-08-self-study-after-follow-through.md), its exact
source/response identities and its September 9 [00:42:46, 01:14:00) UTC window
remain unchanged.

## The chosen repair

Astrid commit `dde5d51136b0822d8172280b4430da8a462c988e` and Minime commit
`378957a9d71fb094ab3b3e66d367900b8c88c09e` are integrated and pushed to their
respective main branches. Both study adapters use the shared reader's explicit
current-input scope: source page, map, search, EOF or recovery. The notebook is a
recalled account, with source reopening/resumption commands. Journals distinguish
input-delivery verification from response accuracy; peer studies retain their
authorship and original correspondence provenance.

Astrid's saved-reading heading names the retained source, revision and actual
admitted interval. New terminal-bearing overflow keeps the original plus a
separate readable view; only the spectral section's terminal controls are removed.
This is a linear text conversion, not a reconstruction of cursor movement or a
semantic summary. Both streams have independent byte positions and hashes.
`READ_MORE RAW` deliberately opens the retained original, parking the readable
bookmark; `ACTIVITY_STATUS` provides its return command. Existing raw offers keep
their original revision and offsets.

The change adds no compulsory note fields, report format, model call or service.
Notebook text is still bounded and can be truncated; labels do not guarantee
claim accuracy. Search quoting remains a separate lower-priority observation.

The preceding Astrid integration commit `d1baeba204` preserves pending Claude
steward work separately. Its review qualifies an unrelated historical claim about
rejected-response retention; it does not change dialogue acceptance policy.
