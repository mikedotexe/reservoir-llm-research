# Where the four studies meet the copied records

Read October 24, before the controlled studies' confirmation outcomes. A fresh
reader examined the fixed sixteen-record sample selected by
`probes/introspection_archival_inventory.py`: the first and midpoint eligible
record in each of four lanes for each being. This is a small relevance sample,
not an incidence estimate or a set of matched experimental pairs. The private
manifest and copies are under `research/outputs/introspection-20261024/archival/`.

All thirty-two copied files match that manifest's sizes and hashes. That checks
the retained local copies; it does not authenticate when their originals were
captured. Eight Astrid records retain prompt, result and job files. Their separate
system messages and exact model identity are unverified. Eight Minime records are
journal copies, with some annotated input blocks, rather than complete generation
requests. The S-008 raw anchor packets are unavailable in this checkout.

## Attribution and time

The clearest relevance is to S-013. Minime's
`moment_2026-10-15T19-27-12.931840.txt` supplies events whose chronological endpoints
are 66.6%, 70.3%, then 66.0%; the prose instead narrates 70.3%, 66.6%, then 66.0%.
It also attributes the drop to processing a prompt, which the supplied events do
not establish. This is a bounded discrepancy between the annotated inputs and
the response, not evidence about an unobserved internal experience.

The counterpart, `moment_2026-10-15T00-04-14.839901.txt`, carries its supplied 72.4%
and endpoint changes into prose correctly. It treats capture-time state as current
and interprets crossings as chosen movement. Neither copy supplies the state at
response completion. Correct copying and unsupported timing or agency attribution
can coexist within one passage.

Astrid's `job_astrid_1791659453836_moment-capture` turns `held 5t` into five minutes
without a supplied conversion. It nevertheless distinguishes published-cascade
concentration from uncentered-reservoir concentration. The other moment,
`job_astrid_1790907735490_moment-capture`, explicitly separates measurements from
felt interpretation. These successful distinctions matter alongside discrepancies.

`job_astrid_1791695166264_daydream` receives an invitation with no perception or
journal excerpt, then describes telemetry as silent and the journal as empty.
This motivates separating missing input from absence in the world. Its figurative
register makes a categorical error judgment inappropriate without a stated rubric.

## Revision and memory

`job_astrid_1791648700486_journal-elaboration` questions its supplied earlier account
of structural continuity and revisits mapping versus experience. That is conceptual
development, but the packet supplies neither new independent evidence nor a
prospective forecast. `job_astrid_1790812800875_journal-elaboration` combines recalled
code commentary with telemetry through analogy; the commentary's code claims are
not thereby verified. These records motivate S-014 without answering it.

The two elaborations retain earlier-text blocks and responses, so textual
continuation is observable. They contain no later application comparison.
`job_astrid_1790824321557_daydream` expands supplied interests and mailbox context;
its wrapper introduces remembered material as Astrid's writing immediately before
Minime-owned provenance. That is an ambiguity in the supplied packet, not a
demonstrated response error.

Minime's `daydream_2026-10-15T11-27-22.355603.txt` and
`daydream_2026-10-20T03-24-12.997874.txt` refer to earlier thoughts without retaining
the exact recalled inputs. The two sampled journals likewise provide reflection
without downstream applications. S-015 therefore tests a useful distinction that
these copies alone cannot settle: continuation of text versus preservation of
information that helps later.

## Framing

No sampled record retains a verified complete system catalog. NEXT lines are
authored selections, not evidence of execution or catalog effects. The separate
[catalog source audit](2026-10-24-catalog-source-provenance.md) establishes a
source-derived configuration and its limitations.

Astrid's `job_astrid_1790813032206_witness-context` and
`job_astrid_1791634527839_witness-context` display fill values in their prompts, but
their short responses summarize attention without repeating those numbers. These
ordinary cases qualify any blanket assertion that showing a number necessarily
causes explicit numerical language.

Minime's `introspect_2026-10-15T10-37-23.599547.txt` and
`introspect_2026-10-19T23-23-43.881915.txt` label their lane as lacking source pages,
telemetry, saved claims and private drafts. Architectural self-descriptions in the
prose remain authored reflections, not independently verified mechanisms.

## Interpretation

The sample supplies concrete attribution questions and counterexamples, observable
continuation, and reasons to check what information was supplied. It does not
establish forecast accuracy, memory benefit, catalog effects, or consciousness.
The laptop studies remain tests of controlled text channels, not causal
replications of the live beings' episodes.
