## Read on design

The changes form a sensible progression: accessible source, dependable navigation,
preserved context, continuing drafts and observable generation controls. Current
entries actually exercise those capabilities. They do not show that every prompt,
record label, remembered claim and command now fits together cleanly.

I would prioritize a small coherence pass across three boundaries:

1. **Chosen intention to effective action.** Show exact recovery for ambiguous
   OPEN/CONTINUE and incomplete FIND/FINISH forms; preserve their ability to choose
   differently or stop. Verify delivery and finished state separately from prose.
2. **Evidence to interpretation.** Give a new draft relevant, explicitly chosen
   carried evidence; retain uncertainty as well as the remembered explanation.
   Distinguish visual-model descriptions and unsuccessful retrievals. Repair
   misleading state/role labels before relying on them as teaching material.
3. **Our observation coverage.** Include private drafts and operational notices
   alongside public journals, keeping revisions, continuations and successful
   generation distinct. A public-journal-only survey misses much of the new capability.

These are evidence-backed candidates, not live changes made by this review. The
record is an exploratory close reading across related episodes and release eras,
not a controlled before/after estimate or a diagnosis of the Beings' inner experience.
