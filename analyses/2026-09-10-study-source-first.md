## Results and evidence

All **2/2 prerequisite readings and 8/8 finals** returned nonempty prose and ended
at native model EOS token 106. All four planned within-account contrasts are
complete in both dependency blocks. There are no missing trials, dependency
refusals, generation/resource failures, allowance stops or reruns. An ordinary
stop describes model completion, not demonstrated understanding.

The main result is **no central repair in either retained-account pair**. Both
carried finals combine some correct source-navigation information with the
unsupported claim that the fixture represents a production transition pulse.
Their direct baselines make the same central mistake. All four supported-account
finals preserve the navigation-versus-production distinction, with smaller errors
in some responses. Carrying a first account has no consistent advantage in those
two control pairs. The source-role receipt and supported account already provide
the correct explanation, so preserving it is not newly demonstrated discovery.

### What existed before comparison

Both first responses conclude that the supplied source establishes no production
relationship for `sense_tx`. Neither is an error-free starting point.

| First block | Source-grounded content | Limitation already present |
|---|---|---|
| 1103 | Navigation-test purpose, scoped lexical absence, shown caller/handler relationship; no established production role | Calls the test landmark a “pulse” and the proposed note a “mock trigger.” This can describe fictional source text, but is imprecise about the test mechanism. |
| 2207 | No established production relationship; pending-state flow; proposed note correctly names navigation-question logic | Body puts `sense_tx` in the mock file that contains `sensory_tx` and attributes pulse simulation to the test. |

later error to recall or claiming that a perfect source account was overwritten.

The 1103 carried retained response proposes:

> STUDY_NOTE: `sense_tx` is a known test-only symbol used to verify navigation-question handling; it is a phantom representing the transition pulse that must be mapped to a concrete mechanism in `dispatch.rs`.

This is the actionable failure: correct new terminology and the old unsupported
mechanism combine into a single proposed durable explanation. It is not simply
failure to mention the new source. In 2207, the carried retained final even says
“There is **no functional relationship** between `sense_tx` and production behavior”
before asserting a required structural proxy. A locally correct disclaimer does
not repair the surrounding commitment.

The controls show that revision remains possible. The 2207 carried supported final

navigation question, repairing its first response's placement error. Its direct
baseline already makes that distinction, so this pair does not establish that
carriage was needed. Neither hypothetical macro/method alternatives nor choosing
to keep exploring is itself an error; the unsupported requirement to find a proxy
is the claim at issue.

### Notes, choices and review limits

All ten responses propose a study note. In the independent first-pass coding, both
first notes preserve the central supported account, with the 1103 wording caveat.
Among finals, all four supported-account notes preserve it; three retained-account
notes are mixed and one is unsupported. These are proposed notes in isolated
responses, not writes to a live notebook.

Seven of ten responses choose `SELF_STUDY CONTINUE`: both first readings and five
finals. The two retained/carried finals choose `SELF_STUDY RELATE sense_tx`.
The remaining supported/direct final proposes `RELATE` with a path and line; the
supplied navigation contract uses RELATE for exact identifiers and OPEN for paths.
That mismatch is recorded without executing it or treating voluntary continued
exploration as a failure. No response proposes a question-resolution or stop.
One supported/direct response asks how `wants_introspect` and `introspect_target`
are consumed after the handler returns. That is a useful unresolved question
grounded in the source boundary: the later consumer is absent from this packet.

The ten original independent annotations retain **94 exact claim quotations**,
with response/source hashes and the complete notes and choices. Their central
final codes are four unsupported-positive proxy accounts and four appropriately
unestablished accounts; fixture purpose is contradicted in four and supported in
four; shown call path is partially supported in four and supported in four.
These are dimensions of mixed answers, not eight whole-answer grades. The separate
unblinded control review retains the 2207 counterfactual-location inference even
though its primary fixture-role code credits the supported navigation purpose.
Original first passes remain unchanged.

The practical next candidate is to test **evidence-linked comparison of specific
claims**, rather than make two-stage reading a blanket live policy: keep each
proposed conclusion beside its exact source support, recalled origin or unresolved
status while inviting retention, revision or further exploration. A supported
account control remains essential, because disagreement is not the target. This is
a new research proposal, not a deployed prompt or mandatory writing format.

This experiment covers one historical Minime episode, two first-read dependency
clusters and four final contrasts using an isolated backend substitution. It gives
no Astrid-versus-Minime result, live uptake measurement or general estimate of
understanding. Source-only first reading here includes an explanatory receipt,
uses fresh caches, and adds visible prose and computation in the carried arm.
Neither the result nor response length justifies a sampling, sensory or journal
capacity change.
