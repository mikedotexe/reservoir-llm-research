## Geometry behavior and repairs

**Minime & Astrid → Geometry bookmarks → Open packet** opens an explicitly chosen
`question-geometry-v1` export. The viewer shows captures, predictions, comparisons
and revisions with full authored accounts. Captures retain all 128 coordinates,
frame selection, engine and wall clocks, gaps and exact selected values. Mean-state
RMS comparisons are recomputed from the retained vectors. A numerical match does
not establish a mechanism, experience, prospective prediction or identical
physical state; hashes establish bytes rather than producer identity.

Review found that optional record fields could smuggle a snapshot or substitute
account into a different record kind. The importer now rejects extraneous fields
per kind, and authored text and rendering use the declared kind. Empty snapshots
are also guarded in the view. Semantic mutation tests reseal both changed records
and their dependent references, assert the intended rejection reason, and include
a valid resealed control.

Actual app navigation then exposed a separate lifecycle defect: leaving Geometry
discarded the imported packet. `ScopeWorkspace` now owns a memory-only view model.
Returning preserves the packet, selected record, frame, coordinate disclosure and
error, including when its source file is no longer available. Cancelled or rejected
replacement preserves the current reading position; a successful replacement
resets it. Closing the window does not create automatic packet persistence.

The [durable synthetic fixtures](../native/ReservoirScope/Tests/Fixtures/geometry-bookmarks/README.md)
include an independent deterministic generator and hash provenance. They qualify
the viewer without executing sibling readers or adapters, accessing live sources,
or calling models. This pass makes no new producer-interoperability claim.
The [viewer guide](../native/ReservoirScope/docs/GEOMETRY-BOOKMARKS.md) and
[dated inquiry](../research/inquiries/2026-09-21-geometry-bookmarks.md) retain the
research limitations and separate runtime rollout requirements.
