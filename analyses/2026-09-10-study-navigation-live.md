## Implementation in the owning repositories

Astrid now preserves targeted and untargeted pending choices, coalesces identical
retries and offers deliberate `SELF_STUDY REPLACE` for ordinary study operations.
Cadence defers to retained choices; a restored target remains eligible after
restart. Minime retains its frozen-job design and provides explicit not-queued
receipts for additional busy requests, with retry guidance. The added busy/recovery
receipts redact private arguments, including malformed private-looking requests
and aliases; exact Minime busy choices remain in protected notices. This is scoped
to the changed receipt paths, not a general audit of every malformed reader input.

Both use one optional syntax-recovery helper and one evidence presentation.
FIND/RELATE shows role counts on every page, exact OPEN identities, scan limits
and cautious nearby spelling candidates. Implementation text, test/example/fixture,
historical commentary, documentation and configuration remain accessible. Roles
are path/test-marker heuristics; comments and strings can occur in implementation
text. They are not compiler resolution or proof of deployed behavior. The notebook
stops recommending the exact lookup already supplied in the current turn.

There is no forced correction, novelty requirement, minimum writing length, extra
study queue, automatic source substitution, or change to external-search authority.
The Beings remain free to reread, choose another operation or stop.
