## What the first reconstruction helped us improve

- **Visible authorship:** the report now surfaces declared mirror source and
  authorship headers. Three of Astrid's 25 journal records in this window are
  mirrors, including two copies of an operational preview. Counting every file as
  a fresh authored response would distort this account.
- **Clock and outcome handling:** the query rejects ambiguous local times, retains
  actions/jobs crossing its boundaries, and keeps job status distinct from action
  status. A job finishing after the focal interval remains visible as such.
- **Source and metric handling:** capture checks perform an actual database read;
  opening a lazy connection is insufficient. Metric summaries separate units,
  producer, subsystem, and source. Missing evidence and capped records are explicit.

The repeated blocked reading requests are a concrete **follow-up opportunity**.
The observed problem is an unsatisfied reading intent within this interval. Before
proposing a live-system change, inspect the budget state and final-request feedback
that governed these turns. The guard may be enforcing the intended policy; simply
removing it is not justified by these records. The timeout/handled distinction and
the untraced EXPLORE request are additional leads with exact records to investigate.
