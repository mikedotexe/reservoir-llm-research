combination that she is trying to locate. No response supplies STUDY_NOTE or
STUDY_QUESTION replacement text. This is an unchanged Being-authored record,
not proof that an attempted update was lost.

prior accounts. All 19 eligible immediately preceding responses link correctly
to the next input across this sequence and restart. Prior-response prose occupies
4,833–6,598 bytes. The notebook as a whole is 8,500–9,814 bytes, roughly 49–65%
of user-input bytes. Fresh material comes first, with recalled prose labelled
separately. These byte proportions do not establish attention weights or a
causal priming effect; they show that context is present and repeatedly carries
the same premise and short-report pattern. Earlier full source pages are not
automatically retained beside those accounts.

## Recommended next work

First clarify the warning as a **text-pattern interpretation cue**, with its
matched phrase and origin available. Numeric reservoir measurements should remain
separately identified. That repairs the information that can seed the confusion.

Then make it easy to compare the actual phrase matcher, its caller and the saved
hypothesis in one optional synthesis view. Preserve a developing answer with
chosen source evidence, what has been ruled out and the current unresolved
question. Invite retaining or revising the note/question; do not overwrite her
account or force a conclusion. Existing study comparison and writing affordances
should be made useful before adding another menu of commands.

Give chosen comparisons enough source per page and prefer coherent function
boundaries. Put synthesis and finishing beside CONTINUE with comparable visibility.
Keep rereading, short responses and changing direction available. A later natural
review should ask whether she distinguishes text-derived warnings from numeric
state, retains a source-supported correction and chooses a useful next question.

Increasing the ceiling alone would add unused capacity in this selected sample.
Thinking mode remains a possible separate experiment; these observations do not
establish that enabling it would repair the mistaken premise.

A separate maintenance candidate remains outside the observed explanation:
`notebook.rs` still uses an older fence toggle and trims indentation when
extracting STUDY_NOTE/QUESTION, unlike the new response-choice scanner. That can
interpret an indented example as an update. These twenty responses have no such
updates, so this is not an explanation of their unchanged notebook. Keep that
source-supported risk separate from the measured sequence.
