# S-006 · When does journal signal become a useful change?

Started: 2026-09-07 Pacific · Lead: Mike with Codex · RQ-04, RQ-05, RQ-07
Board tag: `id:t-flywheel-signal-trace`

## Question and scope

Mike asks when a being's journal contained a signal that another AI agent
judged reasonable to act on, and whether the resulting change measurably helped.
This study treats the whole journal → steward → software → later experience
path as a research object. It does not attribute all downstream work to the
being, or treat an agent's acceptance as independent confirmation.

First pass: exploratory provenance and feasibility, not a causal trial.
Find the flywheel implementation, dated attribution conventions and existing
outcome records; preserve a bounded Git-message census and inspect contrasting
cases. The census covers pinned local Git tips from May 1 through September 7,
2026 (UTC cutoff September 8, 03:15). Main/HEAD and other locally known branch
histories remain separately identified; no remote fetch is performed.

## Material and approach

Sources are the sibling repositories' Git objects, feedback-to-change ledger,
specific stewardship packets, exact source reports and the relevant code/tests.
The subject named in an introspection filename is often the code being read:
`introspection_minime_*` can be Astrid reading Minime. Authorship comes from the
report/generation evidence, never that substring alone.

Use a claim/change episode with many-to-many links, preserving:

| Stage | Required evidence and distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Original signal | Authored passage and hash; being, journal/report identity and date; prompt/source exposure where recoverable. Separate authored content and runtime footer. |
| Selection and review | Queue/run identity, reviewer/model if recorded, exact quote, assessment, contradiction and reason to act. Distinguish unattended rounds from interactive or Mike-requested work. |
| Response | Proposal, characterization test, production change, new observation or deliberate no-change. Preserve original finding separately from issues discovered by the reviewer. |
| Recording | Commit SHA, actual diff, author/committer time, archival or correction role. Quotes/trailers are producer declarations until verified. |
| Activation | Exact deployed revision/process and activation time, or explicitly no runtime change. Commit time is not deployment time. |
| Outcome | Reproduced defect, fixed-case result, natural exposed opportunities, behavioral/operational measurements and later uncoached writing as separate endpoints. |

The initial cases are selected for inspectable links and contrasting response
types, not randomly. This pass makes no estimate of useful-entry prevalence or
flywheel precision. Include incorrect mechanisms, already-covered tests,
unprocessed material, failed rounds and changes with unknown outcomes in any
later rate study. Count episodes and opportunities, not just positive commits.

## Finish line

A source map, repeatable bounded census and concrete cases distinguishing
accepted concern, verified defect, implemented response, deployment and benefit.
Any test-count totals inherited from old packets are labelled historical claims
unless independently rerun. No live change, service operation, induced report or
research delivery to a being is part of this work.
