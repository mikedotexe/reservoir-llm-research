## Judgments and finish line

Code identifies opportunities, origins and persistence. An evidence-coded review must distinguish local prose correction, explicit retraction, saved revision, later supplied revision, later accurate use and reassertion. For the historically saved-note case, the narrow label “durable correction observed” requires an explicit source-supported saved revision and its presence at the next two selected relevant exposures without reassertion. The prose-only worker case cannot acquire that label merely through a later changed note. Exact source and response references accompany every judgment.

The successful finish is a complete bounded account of these two selected cases, including unchanged accounts or no eligible exposure. It is not an accuracy percentage, natural correction rate or causal effect of one release. Repeated responses are dependent observations; shared prompt hashes do not identify individual helper binaries.
