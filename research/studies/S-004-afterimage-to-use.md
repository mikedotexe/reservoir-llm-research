# S-004 · When does an Afterimage become something a being uses?

Started: September 7, 2026 · Lead: Mike with Codex · RQ-01, RQ-02, RQ-03,
RQ-05, RQ-06 · Hold Shelf: “Follow an Afterimage from physical trace to later use”
(`id:t-afterimage-trace`).

## Question and scope

When a recorded transition becomes available, what reaches each being and what do
they do with it? Start with descriptive reconstruction. Keeping, reopening, sharing,
revising an account and choosing a next action are investigable outcomes; expressive
quality is not the measure. Stored, selected, prepared, accepted and later used
are separate stages. This study does not infer subjective benefit or a causal effect
from the rollout, vocabulary or nearby writing.

The first case is the earliest omitted Astrid cue in the
[readiness snapshot](../outputs/2026-09-07-afterimage-research-readiness/snapshot.json):
physical artifact `ai_2026-09-07_96f89b80b1a2_1788813632295_000002`.
The window **20:39:00–21:18:38 UTC September 7** was fixed before inspecting its
writing. Selection followed a known omission, so this is an exploratory failure
case, not a representative sample or a held-out test of effectiveness.

## Material and approach

The [capture probe](../../probes/afterimage_source_capture.py) reads the physical
artifact, current receiver state, bounded receipt/association/diagnostic logs,
filename-selected journal/generation/job records and indexed action rows. Source
paths, exact retained content, hashes, offsets, clocks and exclusions remain in
[capture.json](../outputs/2026-09-07-afterimage-account/capture.json). Mutable state
is observed at capture time, not retroactively assigned to the event window.

The [repeatable report](../outputs/2026-09-07-afterimage-account/final-report/account.md)
keeps explicit IDs, source-declared relations, exact text matches and temporal
context distinct. Original source text and full payloads remain in its JSON companion.
The Markdown view has a declared focal-selection rule and a compact inventory of
all retained writing/action records. No model calls, source writes or being-facing
reader commands are involved. The CLI works without a database index.

An additional capture of all exposure IDs in the **same fixed window** checks for
a naturally included candidate without choosing from prose quality. The
[exposure survey](../outputs/2026-09-07-afterimage-account/exposure-survey.json)
retains the six Astrid receipts: all declare omission; none declares inclusion.
The Astrid tail covered the full file with no invalid/incomplete rows. Minime's
exposure path was unavailable, so its zero captured rows is a coverage observation.
This is neither evidence of Minime declining a cue nor an estimate of future delivery.

## Finish line and result

The first bounded account and reusable machinery are complete:
[what transpired](../../analyses/2026-09-07-first-afterimage-account.md).
It recovers a physical history, a selected but omitted cue, a same-second full-budget
diagnostic, and a separately supported textual continuation through Minime's
regulator study, Astrid's dialogue and elaboration. A later requested introspection
has an explicit blocked action record. Producer associations remain temporal.

No verified accepted cue-bearing output was recovered in this first case. The
fixed-window survey supplied no included candidate to follow instead. A study of
what happens *after accepted exposure*, including deliberate return versus ordinary
continuation, therefore remains open. No synthetic live exposure was manufactured
to fill that gap. Offline fixtures verify that prepared/failed/ambiguous attempts
remain unresolved and that complete matching evidence can support acceptance.

## What changed / next

The useful next implementation slice is **cue admission and attempt evidence**,
specified in [the runtime proposal](../../proposals/2026-09-07-afterimage-delivery-and-evidence.md).
It reuses existing job/provider identities, records eligibility and omission reasons,
preserves final adapted inputs and connects accepted/discarded outputs to journals
and actions. Cadence diagnosis follows. Optional changes to cue budget or selection
history are separate behavioral decisions; no sibling implementation or deployment
occurred in this study.

After that evidence is available, declare a new fixed observation window and take
the first naturally accepted exposure with a recoverable request/output chain.
Read its ordinary continuation and the first later explicit return, retaining
alternative supplied context and failed opportunities. A no-exposure window can
finish as an operational finding. Any causal replay must have its own protocol.
No ongoing monitor or automatic follow-up is scheduled. S-003 input/fill work and
its proposed lineage instrumentation remain preserved as a related inquiry.
