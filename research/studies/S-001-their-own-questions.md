## Question and scope

What questions and unresolved distinctions appear in Astrid's and Minime's writing?
What happens to those questions in nearby entries: elaboration, investigation,
revision, recurrence, or no visible continuation?

The active focus is now **how uncertainty becomes usable knowledge**. We follow
one question through proposed investigation, actual routing/outcome, available
feedback, and later use or revision. These are evidence distinctions, not a score
or a necessary linear sequence. A blocked action, a useful reformulation, or an
unresolved gap can each be informative.

Mike's accepted [research decision](../decisions/2026-09-06-uncertainty-to-usable-knowledge.md)
records the change in direction. [I-001](../inquiries/I-001-breathing-investigation.md)
owns the first source trail: the proposed breathing-dynamics investigation.
We are not rating evocative prose or estimating subjective experience.

This exploratory reading study improves the research agenda and helps us learn
what an episode needs to contain. It estimates no corpus-wide rates and makes no
claim of privileged introspective access.

## Active inquiry protocol · revision 2

1. Select an expressed uncertainty and a concrete proposed investigation. Record
   the being's wording separately from our research question and selection route.
2. Declare a bounded source window and exact reference leads before expanding the
   search. I-001 starts with the named script and September 6, 23:06–23:30 UTC.
3. Follow the records: proposal, requested action, actual dispatch/routing, result,
   whether that result was retained or supplied later, and any visible use/revision.
   Temporal proximity and repeated wording are leads; explicit identifiers or
   source records are required for stronger links.
4. State each claim as supported, contradicted, or unknown, with its source and
   scope. A blocked action establishes a routing outcome, not inability to learn.
   Available feedback, delivered context, and used evidence need separate records.
5. Finish the bounded dossier with what is established, where the trace stops,
   and the next source that could resolve that gap. Keep missing feedback distinct
   from feedback that was available or supplied, with no later use observed in the
   searched records.

The [inquiry template](../templates/inquiry.md) and `trail` tool support this work.
The tool finds literal references in cached raw journals, including action tails,
and cached generation records; it does not classify learning or join a causal chain.
Primary action evidence is preserved separately in the source trace.

## Deferred discovery pilot · revision 1

The following is a proposed reading budget, not an observed sample size:
**12 focal entries, six from each being**, plus their local context. This is small
enough for close reading and deliberately too small for prevalence claims.

1. First record a completed date window, eligible prose lanes, backend/contract era,
   available sources, and file-list ordering. Use one comparable era per being where
   possible; record asymmetries. Exclude machine-generated reports from focal prose,
   while retaining them as context. Record the choice before reading candidates.
2. Per being choose two curated focal entries: one that caught Mike's attention
   (a saved flag or his explicit selection) and one question-bearing entry found by
   the collaborator. Record the selection route and search terms. E-001 can occupy
   one curated slot; its original source has now been recovered.
3. Per being choose four ordinary focal entries from the eligible, non-curated list
   without reading their bodies first. Sort by generation time, then path; for list
   length N choose zero-based positions floor((i + 0.5) * N / 4), i = 0..3.
   If fewer than four are eligible, keep all and report the smaller sample.
   This is temporal coverage, not random sampling or a representative estimate.
4. Include the two preceding and two following eligible prose entries for each focal
   entry, marking unavailable neighbors. Retrieve exact prompt/action links where
   available. Record overlapping windows so one event is not counted as several.
5. Keep focal entries with no question. Do not replace them after reading. Missing
   records stay in the selection log with a reason; any added case is labeled an
   addition. Timed-out generations and intentional silence are distinct when records
   allow that distinction; a missing journal alone cannot distinguish them.

For every selected entry record: source path/ID, event time and timezone, being,
lane, backend/era if known, selection route, context IDs, source availability, and
any exclusions. A Markdown table in this study is enough for the pilot. We do not
need to build a corpus index before this reading session.

## Competing possibilities

A recurring question may be an ongoing inquiry, a repeatedly retrieved cue, a
standing prompt request, or a newly generated variation. Question language may
also be rhetorical. Source tracing and neighboring actions can refine these
interpretations, but partial context can leave them unresolved.

Treat "no follow-up in this window" literally. It does not mean forgotten,
abandoned, or never acted on. The pilot may reveal that the episode window is too
short; document that and design a separate follow-up.
