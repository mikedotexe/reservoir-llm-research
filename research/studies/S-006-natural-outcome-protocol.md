## Opportunity and outcome

The broad screening unit is an occurrence of an exact known marker in retained
provider input/output. The focal opportunity is a marker whose reference is
preserved by the new annotation scan and removed by the old exact scanner:
bracketed relation after a skipped annotation, or one of the added en dash,
em dash or ellipsis suffixes on an annotation. Determine eligibility with the
retained before/after source on identical text; do not infer authorial intent.

For eligible natural **pre-normalization** input, record actual marker retention
at the scanner/output boundary. A post-normalization preserved occurrence can
demonstrate survival, but its absence cannot establish that no input opportunity
existed. Report that ascertainment limitation explicitly. Quoted code and
self-study examples are natural output occurrences, separately classified from
ordinary conversational uses and from induced test strings.

Record denominators: files/attempts read, responses available, marker-bearing
responses, focal eligible occurrences, linked actual outcomes and unknowns.
Separate old/new process, lane, backend/model, contract and output status.
Do not pool unequal windows into a causal rate comparison. Isolated replay of
real retained text holds input fixed but is not a full live counterfactual.

Expected result: eligible references survive under the replacement. Alternatives
include no eligible occurrence, unavailable input/outcome records, or failure
despite the intended release. Zero opportunities or a recording gap completes
this bounded pass without establishing operational benefit. Fewer complaints,
generic prose changes, test counts and logging volume are not benefit endpoints.
