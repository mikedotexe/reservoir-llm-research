## Question and alternatives

S-018's facts-only arm often quoted correct facts while choosing the wrong route
under a changed rule. S-019's direct baseline and both check formats succeeded,
but its task text and banks also changed. We first repeat the original facts-only
requests unchanged. If their errors recur at both seeds in multiple worlds, test
whether adding a public comparison before the action improves simulated utility.
Compare the same check after the action to distinguish the requested order from
the bundle of extra fields and instructions. A null, reversal, ceiling, or failure
to reproduce is a useful possible endpoint; none authorizes prompt hunting.
