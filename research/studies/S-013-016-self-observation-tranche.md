# Four studies of useful self observation

Started October 24, 2026. Mike selected this tranche after reviewing the literature
and approved implementation and autonomous iteration. Subject: isolated laptop
language-model experiments. The live beings, their services, the frozen desktop
0.39.0 instrument, and private activation interventions are outside this tranche.

## Questions and sequence

1. S-013: distinguish subject, measurement time, displayed value, and unavailable
   current state in supplied observations.
2. S-014: predict whether a later interpretation is supported by new evidence,
   comparing self-framed and observer-framed forecasts with equal visible inputs.
3. S-015: compare original reflection, faithful paraphrase, factual notebook,
   irrelevant memory and no memory in a later application.
4. S-016: compare source-derived rich action descriptions with a condensed menu,
   a padded condensed menu and neutral context, without executing actions.

The individual prospective protocols specify the cases, branches, scoring and
finish lines. Study completion never requires a positive result. These experiments
test supplied text channels and behavior, not direct access to private activations.

## Shared instrument and invariants

The separate `reservoir_experiments` standard-library Python package snapshots
protocols and explicit inputs, then sends one request at a time to the laptop's
numeric loopback Ollama endpoint. It does not manage services, pull models, execute
NEXT actions, or fall back to another provider. The existing corpus-indexing CLI
and desktop formats are unchanged.

Primary model: `gemma4:12b`, temperature 0.7, thinking disabled, paired seeds 7 and
20260909, context capacity 16384 tokens, output limits 1024 for prose and 256 for
forecasts, total request deadline 180 seconds. Model/template/runtime identities
are bound at execution. Mistral Small 24B uses a separately retained identity and
the same settings, with no unsupported thinking parameter. A preselected twelve-
episode cross-model check uses every main condition, independently of findings.

Development and confirmation remain separate. Allow one documented development
revision for evidence ambiguity, scoring defects, context fit or completion
problems. Retain both versions. Confirmation inputs and primary contrasts are
frozen before their outcomes. Dependent requests render only explicit input
artifacts; forecast completion can order a target without exposing its text.

Requests, responses, physical attempts, failures and unresolved dispatches are
retained privately under `research/outputs/`. Resume dispatches only never-attempted
nodes. A missing ancestor blocks text-dependent descendants, not independent cases.
Model drift, broken evidence, context invalidity or cross-branch leakage stops
dispatch. A negative finding is not a stop reason.

## Reading and analysis

Two fresh readers receive fixed samples without treatment labels or prior
conclusions. Initial readings are retained before disclosure. Fidelity judgments
need the visible evidence; they are allocation-blind, not blind to that evidence.
Unknowns, omissions and unavailable generations remain distinct from incorrect
beliefs. Malformed verdicts can be recovered only by a frozen evidence-coded
adjudication, with original format compliance retained separately.

The unit of replication is the episode. Seed-paired differences are averaged
within episodes before aggregation. `reservoir_experiments.statistics` produces
paired effects and 10000-draw episode-bootstrap intervals (seed 20261024), with
all paired and missingness denominators. These intervals describe this constructed
bank and do not establish population prevalence.

The final archival relevance check uses already copied records only. Exact prompt
availability, source-derived reconstruction and missing provenance remain separate.
The two S-008 anchors and S-012 contrasts are contextual evidence, not live causal
replications of these trials.
