## Try the offline lab

On an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later, follow the
[source build instructions](../native/ReservoirScope/README.md#build-from-source)
to make a local app from this checkout, or open a trusted copy already provided
to you. The app archive is not published in this repository; the qualified local
candidate’s identity and location are [recorded below](#qualified-candidate-identity).
Building now creates a new package with its own source identity.

Open **Guided tour** and begin at **A · Minimal reservoir**.
Use **Continue** to visit useful recorded moments, then follow
[the guided journey](../native/ReservoirScope/docs/GUIDED-TOUR.md) from state into
writing, return, memory and choice. These examples work offline without model
downloads, private research captures or a Mac Mini connection.

For retained evidence walkthroughs, open **Research cases** and choose a question.
For hands-on changes, open **Experiments**. For an invented packet demonstrating
the geometry viewer, open **Geometry bookmarks → Load synthetic example**.
**Observatory** is the fifth direct destination. To exercise the numerical core
without the app, try the [scripted runner walkthrough](../essentials/runner/README.md#scripted-quickstart).
The [synthetic research CLI walkthrough](QUICKSTART.md) is a separate route for
indexing and reading journal evidence.

Normal sessions save experiments and journals under
`~/Library/Application Support/Reservoir Scope/`. Viewing bundled examples creates
no saved-run copy. Opened cases and geometry packets remain in the current window,
including selection and geometry reading position; reopen files in a later window.
**Runs & examples → Import experiment…** verifies and saves a Library copy;
**Open recording…** inspects a file without adding one.
Fresh model generation and live Beings feeds are explicit choices.
**Check local model** makes one bounded loopback inventory request; it does not
promise a successful generation. Replay makes no model request.

## What changed

- Five direct destinations expose the guide, experiments, reviewed cases, geometry
  and recorded observations. Selecting a destination starts no new experiment;
  leaving ongoing experiments pauses them.
- **Continue** is separate from expandable saved-record playback controls. Continue
  jumps to a recorded checkpoint and pauses; collapsing playback also pauses it.
- **Research cases** leads from an existing question to its reviewed account and
  embedded evidence. Background verification preserves the prior valid case and
  selection when a replacement is cancelled or rejected.
- [Geometry bookmarks](../native/ReservoirScope/docs/GEOMETRY-BOOKMARKS.md) adds an
  explicitly synthetic example using the unchanged fixture. Authored accounts,
  all 128 capture coordinates, scrubbing, clocks, gaps and recomputed RMS remain
  inspectable. Invalid replacements preserve the packet, reading position and origin.
- Case and geometry state survive workspace navigation within the window. Opening
  them is read-only and creates no saved experiment; experiment import saves a copy.

The two reviewed cases and their interpretations are unchanged. Explicit-input
daily analysis and private archive tools remain separate. This pass did not execute
sibling producers, read live source systems or make fresh model requests. Producer
interoperability and paired Being rollout are separate claims.
