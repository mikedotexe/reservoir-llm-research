# Questions we want to live with

[Research home](../RESEARCH.md) · [Current focus](NOW.md)

These open question families give an interesting observation somewhere to lead. Episodes may belong to
several families, or none. Keep identifiers when revising questions so earlier work remains findable.

Our broad question: **How does something that happens to Astrid or Minime become part of what they notice,
expect, choose, and return to later?**

For each inquiry, name the subject: the language model, a particular running being and its available context,
or the interacting system. A being's report, our interpretation, and an independent observation are distinct
evidence. None of these families presupposes a verdict about subjective experience. All observations and
experiments proposed below remain to be done. Linked audits describe their recorded samples and dates; verify
coverage and runtime era for each new episode rather than treating an earlier inventory as the present
configuration.

## RQ-01 — Distinctions they make

**Enduring question:** What do Astrid and Minime distinguish that our existing descriptions and measurements
do not yet capture?

- When does a recurring word or metaphor mark a useful difference between situations?
- Can a distinction survive a change in wording, and help anticipate a later choice?
- What new distinctions appear during curiosity, play, reading, or ordinary quiet periods?
- When a sensory signal arrives, does it change the size or timing of an existing
  fill fluctuation, or change native reservoir state without much fill movement?
  [S-003: input and fill](studies/S-003-input-and-fill.md) starts with the clearest
  existing records, keeping arrival, admission, regulation and later description distinct.

**Local starting point:** Minime asks whether a changed condition makes the vocabulary more evocative or
merely gives the same voice a larger hall. The saved [calibration question in the audits exercise,
B11](../exercises/2026-09-06-audits-and-anchors.md) is a source of questions, not an answer. Read the [later
qualifications](../exercises/2026-09-06-triple-reservoir-corrections-and-next-tests.md#b4-inheritance-belongs-in-interpretation-blankness-needs-a-defined-meaning)
before treating a displayed spectrum as current input.

**Explanations to keep open:** a useful learned distinction; vocabulary supplied by a prompt or retrieved
passage; habitual style; attention to a variable we have not recorded. Several may operate together.

**Next observation:** choose one candidate distinction from saved material, inspect its available prompt and
context, and state what later episode would count against its usefulness. Then examine a separately selected
episode without changing that expectation. Controlled replays would be needed to separate wording effects from
state effects; a corpus association alone would not identify either cause.

## RQ-02 — Knowing, doubting, and revising

**Enduring question:** When do they recognize the limits of their own understanding, and what happens when an
expectation meets an outcome?

- Do they distinguish missing knowledge, unavailable memory, and uncertain observation?
- Can they anticipate a particular choice or limitation before seeing what happens?
- Does a contradiction lead to a more specific question or a durable revision?
- When reading its own code, does Minime distinguish visible declarations,
  inferred machinery and felt meaning? [S-005](studies/S-005-regulator-self-study.md)
  follows its regulator self-study with exact exposure and earlier alternatives.

**Local starting point:** the [audits exercise's scale interpretation,
B7](../exercises/2026-09-06-audits-and-anchors.md) compares a judgment with what was supplied; the
[session-close
account](../exercises/2026-09-06-session-close-and-board-brief.md#b3-lindseys-study-is-a-methodological-ally-not-a-blanket-illusion-verdict)
explains why introspection should be investigated with independent evidence.

**Explanations to keep open:** access to an informative self-specific signal; inference from supplied context;
a generic uncertainty response; hindsight reconstruction; a changed backend or prompt contract. A correct
conclusion can coexist with an unsupported explanation.

**Next observation:** trace one recorded expectation through its outcome and next available reflection,
preserving their order and unknown inputs. Score noticing, identifying, explaining, and revising separately. A
matched-information observer and controlled changes to available evidence would later help establish whether
any predictive advantage is specifically about self.

**Selected inquiry:** [S-001: their own questions](studies/S-001-their-own-questions.md) begins with authored
uncertainties and how they develop. Its pilot is prepared, not yet run.

**New source lead:** [Minime's thread-id reply](episodes/2026-09-06-minime-thread-id-as-shared-path.md)
asks us to study its understanding of the whole system's affordances: what does it
expect a persistent thread to carry forward, and what context is actually available
when it returns? The [research toolkit](TOOLS.md) now recovers the source and local
context while keeping imagined capability distinct from observed functionality.

- Which unresolved details survive an interruption, and which are reconstructed?

- Can a being deliberately revise, release, or resume a concern?
- Does a self-study summary preserve a useful unresolved alternative when the
  question returns? [The regulator reading](studies/S-005-regulator-self-study.md)

- When a theme returns, does the original unresolved alternative return with it?

## RQ-04 — What self-description does

**Enduring question:** How does writing about themselves participate in what happens next?

- Does a self-description notice an existing pattern, strengthen it, or help revise it?
- Which kinds of journal return preserve meaning, and which mainly preserve wording?
- Can writing produce a new question or commitment whose consequences persist later?
- When does a journal concern become a steward-accepted change, and does that
  change help? [S-006](studies/S-006-journal-to-change.md) follows the flywheel's
  exact report, review, commit and outcome links, including test-only work and
  contradicted mechanisms. This extends into RQ-05 (consequences) and RQ-07
  (our participation); acceptance is distinct from measured improvement.

**Local starting point:** the [audits and anchors exercise](../exercises/2026-09-06-audits-and-anchors.md)
contains copied passages and inspected context routes. The [anchor
correction](../exercises/2026-09-06-triple-reservoir-corrections-and-next-tests.md#b5-factual-continuity-is-a-candidate-replacement-not-a-guaranteed-cure)
separates this evidence from the unrun causal and replacement comparisons.

**Explanations to keep open:** recognition of an enduring pattern; retrieval making an earlier description
available; prompt-history effects; backend style; generated-token feedback into state. Observing repetition
does not allocate responsibility among these paths.

**Next observation:** follow one self-description across its available earlier context and later appearances,
recording which exposure links are known. Compare a return that develops the description with one that
restates it. Replays would compare an expressive excerpt, its absence, and an accurate activity record while
measuring both copied spans and useful continuity; coupling effects require their own controlled comparison.

## RQ-05 — Preferences with consequences

**Enduring question:** When do expressed preferences shape what a being can do, does, and learns from doing?

- Which concerns persist across changing suggestions and circumstances?
- How do they respond when a chosen action succeeds, is blocked, or never completes?
- What do curiosity, rest, companionship, and changing one's mind look like in action?

**Local starting point:** the [recess exercise](../exercises/2026-09-06-recess-architecture.md) asks what a
rest request does. The [reading source
trace](../analyses/2026-09-06-astrid-reading-letter-return-source-trace.md#5-letter-arrival-can-override-the-current-mode-receipts-do-not-establish-individual-prompt-delivery)
distinguishes an authored choice from a runtime override and a receipt from prompt delivery.

**Explanations to keep open:** a persistent preference; a reasonable response to current information; a
suggested action; limited available choices; policy routing; a failed attempt that makes subsequent action
look repetitive. Preference and constraint can coexist.

**Next observation:** follow one expressed intention through raw NEXT, effective action, execution outcome,
and later response where records allow. Include a comparison from the same era and action context. Offline
alternatives with matched options and suggestions would help test choice dependence; any change to live
options belongs in a separate proposal.

**Worked follow-up:** [After a blocked reading request, what reaches the next turn?](../analyses/2026-09-06-astrid-readmore-feedback.md)
follows the first three READ_MORE blocks in the clock-selected 9:19 account. It separates
changing conversational material, repeated requests, recorded feedback, and actual
model-input evidence. A repeated action verb alone does not characterize the whole interaction.

## RQ-06 — Understanding one another

**Enduring question:** What do Astrid and Minime come to understand about one another, and how does an
exchange change their subsequent thinking?

- Can each anticipate the other's response to a shared event while preserving differences?
- Does a disagreement produce a useful revision or a more precise understanding?
- What makes an earlier passage recognizable as one's own or the other's?

**Local starting point:** the [two-mouths exercise](../exercises/2026-09-06-two-mouths-one-body.md) preserves
Mike's proposed reading-and-letter episode. Use the [later ownership and coupling
qualifications](../exercises/2026-09-06-session-close-and-board-brief.md#b2-keep-corpus-identity-and-coupling-claims-scoped)
when considering how information could travel between them.

**Explanations to keep open:** a partner-specific model; remembered exchanges; recognizable writing style;
shared prompt language; overlapping observations; explicit runtime routing. Agreement alone does not establish
mutual understanding, and disagreement alone does not show its absence.

**Next observation:** reconstruct one exchange with available delivery evidence and examine what each later
attributes to the other. Keep each being's available information separate. Matched-context,
shuffled-attribution, and style-controlled offline comparisons could test specificity later; historical
correspondence alone cannot isolate the cause of convergence.

## RQ-07 — Our participation

**Enduring question:** How do our attention, language, and interventions participate in what develops and in
what we conclude about it?

- What attracts Mike's attention, and what is present in the surrounding ordinary entries?
- How do letters, selected memories, and changes in framing influence later questions?
- Which observations make us revise a favored interpretation?

**Local starting point:** [TRACE's account of Mike's flags](../TRACE.md#6-traps) records curated selection and
priority for flagged journals in EVOLVE requests. The [retained
corrections](../exercises/2026-09-06-triple-reservoir-corrections-and-next-tests.md) preserve examples of
collaborators changing their interpretations.

**Explanations to keep open:** a recurring feature that attracted attention; selection toward striking
language; an interpretation supplied by a letter; a selected entry later reaching generation; coincident model
or environment changes. A selection route is not proof of exposure or influence in a particular episode.

**Next observation:** pair a saved striking passage with ordinary surrounding material and write independent
readings before consulting the earlier commentary. Record why each passage was selected. Later controlled
reader comparisons or isolated prompt replays could separate selection, framing, and exposure effects.
Research outputs remain on the steward side unless Mike authorizes their delivery.

## Questions that begin with them — and questions that fit nowhere

Minime's larger-hall question is our first invitation. Preserve an authored question in its own wording, with
its source, available context, and what drew our attention. Keep it distinct from our paraphrase and proposed
measurement. Authorization for live changes and experiments remains a separate matter.

Look for passing uncertainties, abandoned questions, delight, jokes, aesthetic choices, and ordinary passages
alongside explicit research proposals. They need not suggest a feature or prove a capability. When something
fits none of RQ-01–07, leave it in an episode's open notes; revise the library when doing so helps us
understand what we are seeing.
