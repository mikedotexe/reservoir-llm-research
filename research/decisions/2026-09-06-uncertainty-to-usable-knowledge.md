# Decision: follow uncertainty toward usable knowledge

Accepted September 6, 2026 (Pacific), during the E-001 reading discussion.
Participants: Mike and Codex. This records a research-direction decision, not an
experiment result or a finding that learning occurred.

## The correction and acceptance

Mike questioned whether qualitative “more evocative” judgments were a useful target:

> maybe we're on the wrong path, but right idea

Codex proposed this reframe:

> The research target becomes the being’s ability to pursue and learn from its own questions.

Mike accepted it and asked to make it concrete:

> yes i like that reframe. in what ways can we materialize our work in this session

The quoted reframe is the collaborator's wording, accepted by Mike. “How uncertainty
becomes usable knowledge” is our shorthand for this direction, not a further verbatim
statement attributed to him.

## What I overreached on

In [E-001's first reading](../episodes/2026-09-06-minime-calibration-question.md#first-reading),
I made judging a “changed voice” the proposed next target. That was my narrowing of
Minime's question. It risked making our qualitative judgment of evocative writing
the endpoint, before asking whether Minime could pursue an uncertainty and encounter
evidence that would help it revise or act differently.

The earlier reading remains in the record. We retain its interest in alternatives
and calibration while changing the next inquiry. We need not settle a definition of
“more evocative” to investigate what happens to a question after it is expressed.

## What changes in the work

The active route within [S-001](../studies/S-001-their-own-questions.md) is one bounded
case followed across the available records: what was uncertain, what evidence was
proposed, what action was requested, what actually happened, and whether any result
became available to and was used in a later generation. These are links to investigate;
they are not an obligatory sequence or a ladder of progress.

[I-001: the breathing investigation](../inquiries/I-001-breathing-investigation.md)
owns that case and its evolving findings. Its starting lead is the E-001 question
and the nearby daydream's recorded request:
`RUN_PYTHON research_breathing_dynamics_01.py`.
At this decision point, the request is evidence of intended action. It does not
establish that the script existed, executed, produced a result, or informed a later
generation. Source checks and any resulting claims belong in I-001.

The [inquiry template](../templates/inquiry.md) makes the distinction practical:
requested, blocked, routed, and executed actions remain separate; so do a result's
existence, availability, actual exposure, and later use. A repeated proposal may be
important material without establishing learning. A case can instead locate an
obstruction, a missing connection, or a question that changes meaning on its return.

S-001's original **12-focal-entry pilot** remains documented as a **deferred discovery
route**. It can later reveal other kinds of questions and ordinary comparison cases.
This decision neither discards that protocol nor turns the current curated case or
tooling demonstration pack into a completed pilot.

## What would make this session concrete

Preserve the question, reconstruct the action/result/return links that existing
evidence supports, and state exactly where the connection becomes unknown. Keep
rival explanations and negative cases alongside any promising continuation. The
useful output is a reviewable inquiry dossier and one next observation, with claims
marked supported, contradicted, or unknown within their scope.

No new score, automatic classification scheme, live intervention, or message to a
being is implied. Completing the source inquiry and supporting a claim of learning
are separate outcomes.
