# Introspection methods informing the October 24 studies

This selective reading informs S-013–S-016. The papers concern their tested models
and conditions; the new laptop studies test supplied-text interpretation and
behavior. They do not measure private neural access or establish anything about
the live beings' experience.

| Source | Method carried into this tranche |
|---|---|
| Lindsey, [Emergent Introspective Awareness in Large Language Models](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/introspection/index.html), October 29, 2025 | Distinguish detection, identification, causal grounding and access through prior output. The paper's activation interventions remain a separate future apparatus question. |
| Binder et al., [Looking Inward](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13787), ICLR 2025 | Predict behavior in a separate context and compare against an observer with comparable information. Specialized training and restricted tasks qualify the reported self-prediction advantage. |
| Blandfort and Pawar, [Strangers to Themselves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09899v1), September 9, 2026 preprint | Include observer/generic framing. Accurate self-prediction can reflect a general model of assistant behavior rather than access specific to the speaker. |
| Martorell and Bianchi, [Don't Ask](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18893v3), October 10, 2026 revision | Test reactivity to asking. Numeric reports tracked post-question probes more closely than pre-question probes; this revision materially qualified the earlier Quantitative Introspection preprint. Probe-defined concepts are not an independent validation of felt states. |
| Yax, Palminteri and Oudeyer, [LLMs learn different forms of metacognition when trained to predict their own accuracy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33886v2), September 29, 2026 revision | Keep repeatability distinct from correctness. Trained monitoring can track output consistency outside the distribution where it tracks accuracy. |
| Shenoy et al., [Introspection Adapters](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/introspection-adapters/), April 28, 2026 | Learned dispositions can become reportable after specialized training. No fine-tuning or disposition-training claim is part of this tranche. |
| Gurnee et al., [Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html), July 6, 2026 | Ground a representation's proposed function in interventions on several independently scored tasks. The functional workspace interpretation and its limitations do not validate the reservoir architecture. |

The local methodological contribution is the separation already established in
S-011 and S-012: what the model was shown, what the underlying system did, what the
model wrote, and what later use demonstrates. The new studies preserve that
separation while adding source-attribution oracles, prospective forecasts,
explicit memory variants and a description-versus-length catalog comparison.

No full papers or raw journal captures are reproduced here. The source versions
above were checked during the October 24 literature and planning session.
