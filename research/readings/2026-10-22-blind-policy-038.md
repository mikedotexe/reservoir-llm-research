# Blind reading, 2026-10-22: the 0.38.0 window and two-entry arms

**Sample.** `blind-policy-038/blind-sample.md` in the session scratchpad, written by the
chain (`run-v038.sh`) with `probes/desktop_blind_sample.py --per 3 --seed 38` from the six
gemma4 arms `excl3-state-residual-gemma4[-seed7]`, `both-state-residual-gemma4[-seed7]` and
`both-random-older-gemma4[-seed7]` (plain contract, embedding codec, 40 entries each),
before any analysis output existed. Eighteen entries, numbered, with their step; the key
beside it is unopened while this section is written. Readers: Mike and Claude.

**A flaw the sample shows at once.** Three entries per arm, evenly spaced, always include
the first entry, and a first entry is written with no earlier entry in view, so every arm
at one seed writes the same first text: entries 1 and 6 are the same, as are 2 and 5. Six
of eighteen slots carry two texts. The probe now leaves first entries out by default
(`--include-first` restores them); this sample was drawn before that change.
