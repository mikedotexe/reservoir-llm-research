available. Record a supplied suggestion separately. READ_MORE and source-reader
CONTINUE/RESUME/MAP/FIND/OPEN are distinct actions.

terminal outcome and next delivered page. Label parentage, exact text matches and
temporal neighbors separately. Include blocking feedback and question carryover.

claim, or a concrete next observation. Keep requested repair and deployed change
distinct. No aggregate quality score is needed.
