# HSS-XX · What happened

Recorded date (UTC and Pacific); link to the [SELF_STUDY history](../histories/self-study.md).
Retrospective discovery or predeclared follow-up; Being(s), source owner and channel.

**Problem and evidence.** Preserve an exact journal/prompt passage, action/job IDs,
source/revision and the selection/window. Distinguish prepared material from actual
input, requested NEXT from routing/completion, and source delivery from understanding.

**Decision and implementation.** State what design assumption failed, the smallest
chosen repair, alternatives left open, implementation commits and owning record.
Separate approved proposal, implemented source, merged/pushed code and live release.

**Activation.** Per-Being UTC process start, PID, source/manifest identity and receipt.
Use unknown/pending where evidence is absent. Record other prompt/model/process changes.

**Natural observation.** Freeze a window before a new test; label retrospective
examples exploratory. Retain exact inputs, notebook origins, authored output and
actual follow-through. State new versus repeated bytes, navigation and READ_MORE
separately, with denominators and incomplete observation horizons.

**What follows.** Supported result, counterexample, uncertainty and next observation
or repair. Do not turn a test pass or successful navigation into a comprehension claim.

**Retention/corrections.** Link the research-local evidence manifest and verifier.
Append corrections next to the earlier claim; preserve old captures and their scope.
Update the history, current-focus page and affected proposal disposition. Do not
alter an existing daily ledger or create a schedule merely to add a history entry.
